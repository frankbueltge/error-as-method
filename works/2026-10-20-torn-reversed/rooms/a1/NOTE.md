# The Year Remembered

This folds the twenty years of *The Natural History of Selborne* into a single year. Each dated letter sits as a dot just outside one ring, on the day White wrote it, stepped outward by year from 1767 to 1787. Its size shows its length, its colour shows who it went to (Pennant in rust, Barrington in green), and it is drawn open when written away from Selborne. From each dot, threads cross the ring to every month the letter names in its text, thicker the more often it names that month. A letter that stays with its own season makes a short loop near the rim. One written in winter about the swallows' return reaches across the ring. Around the rim, each month shows how many letters were written in it and how often the book names it. The 32 letters with no date tally their months in a column on the left. On the right, the letters that range over the most months spell out the year they cover.
