# The Year Remembered

The letters are folded into one year: the 21 years of dates are dropped, and each dated letter is set on the day of the year it was written, along an upper calendar line, sized by its length and coloured by its recipient (Pennant in rust, Barrington in green). The letters with no day stand in a small column at the far left. The script then finds every month the letters name, 344 of them, counting feast days such as Michaelmas and Midsummer and leaving out each letter's own dateline. A thread runs from each letter down to the month it names on a second calendar line below: it falls straight when White writes about the season he is in, and slants when he writes back to another one. Under each named month are the creatures and kinds of weather that come up most often near it. The picture shows a correspondence that looks back: the November letters reach across the whole year, and the spring months of swifts, hirundines and nightingales are mostly written about from later in the year.
