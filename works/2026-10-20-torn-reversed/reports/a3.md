I made "The Order of Reading", a variation on "The Turning Year". It uses the original's parsing of the letters and its keyword for each letter, but replaces the spiral with two orders shown at once.

- **Top:** all 110 letters hang in the order the book prints them, Pennant's then Barrington's. Each is a column showing its number, its keyword written upward, and a bar for its length.
- **Threads:** from each bar a thread falls to a 1767–1787 time line and lands on the day the letter was dated. An open ring marks a letter dated away from Selborne.
- **What it shows:** the book prints the two sets of letters one after the other, but White wrote them alongside each other, so the two colours of thread sweep across each other over 1769–1780. Threads that cross within one set show where the book printed a letter out of date order.
- **Undated letters:** a letter with only a year gets a dashed thread ending in a bracket over that year. A letter with no date drops a short dotted thread that stops before reaching the time line.

It is a single still SVG built with plain JavaScript, and it loads only `data/selborne.js` from the folder. The viewer rendered it at 1100 × 800 with no page errors. The files are `index.html` and `NOTE.md`, and the final render is `renders/02.png`.
