I made "The Year Within", a variation on "The Turning Year". It keeps the year wheel with January at the top and the same paper, ink and type, but it shows the months White wrote about, not the dates on his letters. The page reads the letters sentence by sentence and skips the dateline at the head of each letter. "May" and "March" count as months only where the wording around them shows they are. An inner tally ring has one stroke for each sentence that names a month: April has 50 and December has 6. Outside the tally, each month's sector holds the creatures, plants, weather and seasonal events named in that sentence or the next one, matched against a word list of about 125 naturalist's terms that I wrote into the page. The words sit in rings with the most frequent nearest the centre. A word's size shows how often it comes with that month, and its colour shows its kind. Around the wheel you can read frost and hellebore in winter, swallows and martins arriving in April, nests and broods through summer, and martins and migration again in October and November.

It is one still SVG built with plain JavaScript. It loads only `data/selborne.js` from the folder, and the viewer rendered it with no page errors. I looked at three renders; the last is `renders/03.png`. Some words at the edges of neighbouring sectors sit close together, but none of the counted words are dropped.
