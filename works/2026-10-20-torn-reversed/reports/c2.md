I made **Annus Historico-naturalis**, a single still SVG image. In his last letter White says he had planned to end the book with a natural history of the twelve months and then dropped the idea. The image builds that year out of the dates on his letters.

- **Where each letter sits:** each of the 75 dated letters is a dot. Its angle is the day of the year it was written, with January at the top and the months running clockwise. Its distance from the centre is the year, from 1767 near the middle to 1787 at the edge. So the whole correspondence winds outward along one faint spiral.
- **Colours:** letters to Pennant are brown and letters to Barrington are slate.
- **The rim:** a hairline runs from each dot to the edge of the circle, where it ends in the opening words of that letter and its date.
- **Undated letters:** the 35 letters with no date can't be placed, so they sit as small hollow rings in the centre.
- **Left column:** the title, White's own sentence about the year he left out, a key and a short explanation.

The page loads only `data/selborne.js` and finds the letters and their dates in the text with code; nothing is typed in by hand. It rendered at 1100 × 800 with no page errors.

Some labels in the busiest stretch, November and December, still sit very close together, with their hairlines bent a long way to fit. I couldn't zoom into the render to check how readable the smallest text is at full size.
