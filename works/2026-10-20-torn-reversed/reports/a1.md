I made "The Year Remembered", a variation on the spiral of dated letters. It folds all twenty years into one ring. Each of the 78 dated letters is a dot on the day White wrote it, stepped outward by year. From each dot, 143 threads in total cross the ring to the months that letter names in its own text, so a letter written in winter about spring reaches across the year. The letters keep the original's colours (Pennant in rust, Barrington in green), and dot size still shows length. Around the rim, each month shows how many letters were written in it and how often the book names it. A column on the left tallies the months named by the 32 undated letters, and a small table on the right lists the letters that range over the most months.

To pick up "May" without catching the verb, the match only counts it after words like "of" or "in", or before a date number. Some mentions could still be miscounted. January is named unusually often by the undated letters; I didn't check why.

It is a single still SVG page that loads only `data/selborne.js`, and it renders with no errors. The files are `index.html` and `NOTE.md`, and the latest picture is `renders/02.png`.
