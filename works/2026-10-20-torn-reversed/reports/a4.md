I made "The Year Remembered", a variation on "The Turning Year". The original spiral is replaced by two straight calendar lines, January to December, with the years dropped. On the upper line each dated letter is a dot on the day of the year it was written, sized by its length and coloured by recipient (Pennant in rust, Barrington in green). The letters with no day sit in a small column at the far left.

The script finds every month named in the letters' text, 344 in all. That count includes feast days such as Michaelmas and Midsummer and leaves out each letter's own dateline. Each mention gets a thin curved thread down to the month it names on the lower line. A straight drop means White is writing about the season he is in; a slant means he is writing back to another one. Under each named month are the creatures and kinds of weather mentioned most often near it, such as swifts, hirundines and black-cap in April, or migration and ring-ousels in September.

What the picture shows: the November letters reach across the whole year, and the spring months are mostly written about from later in the year.

It is a single still SVG with no libraries, and it loads only `data/selborne.js`. The last render shows no page errors.
