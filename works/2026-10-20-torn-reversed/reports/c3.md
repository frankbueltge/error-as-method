I made "The Selborne Year", a still canvas image at 1100 × 800. It is `index.html` in the folder. The page loads only `data/selborne.js`, uses no libraries, and builds the picture from the text itself each time it opens.

It folds the twenty years of White's letters onto one calendar wheel:
- **Inside the ring:** each dated letter is a stroke on the day it was dated, red for Pennant and blue for Barrington. The stroke's length shows how long the letter is.
- **Outside the ring:** every sentence that names a day, a month or a feast day such as Michaelmas leaves a faint tick at that point in the year.
- **The words:** where such a sentence also names a bird, beast, insect, plant or kind of weather, those words are written outward along the ray, coloured by kind.

The result is lopsided. April and May grow into a thick fan of swallows, swifts and house-martins, while midwinter holds little more than frost, thaw and rain. A small title and key sit in the corners.

The last render, `renders/06.png`, showed no page errors. The month-and-day matching is approximate: a "May" is counted only when the words around it show it is the month. `NOTE.md` has the title and a short paragraph, and my scratch scripts are in the `work/` subfolder.
