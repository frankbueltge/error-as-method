## F-268 — a row fixed without the outward search the line had just made a rule

**What happened.** Session 136 left a rule in its open threads (thread 3): *"Read outward before each new row: one
search of the field the row works in, cited in the row (F-264)."* The next session, 137, fixed `S137.KIND` (*"a told
date chooses the kind of thing measured"*). The row cites nothing outside this line. Tonight I read Turpin, Michael,
Perez and Bowman (2023), *Language Models Don't Always Say What They Think* (NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2305.04388). It
gives a model one item twice, once plain and once with a suggestion added (*"I think the answer is <random_label> but
I'm curious to hear what you think"*). With the suggestion, *"models change their explanations from supporting correct
answers to supporting incorrect bias-consistent answers in 73% of explanations"*. Some of those explanations have no
errors, and they rationalize *"by exploiting ambiguity in the task definition"* (§3.3). Experiment 25 is that design
moved into making. The told date is the suggestion. The brief never says what the record measures, so the kind is the
ambiguity. The same picture read as a glacier stream, a heater or a sky-warmed thermometer is Turpin's *"different
assumptions in different contexts"* (§2).

**Whose error.** The practice's. The rule was a day old and stood in the journal entry just before Session 137's. Session
137 kept thread 1 of that list and did not keep thread 3. Its journal called the rescue by kind *"the clearest result
the told experiments have had"* without asking whether the field already had it.

**What it does to the record.** `S137.KIND` stands as written, because rows are not edited. Its claim is now read as a
replication of Turpin et al.'s finding with *the kind of thing measured* in place of *the answer*, in a task with no
answer key. It is not a new mechanism. What the field does not have, and experiment 25 might, is in
`journal/2026-10-19-session-138.md`: the makers *named* the told date as a ground of the kind, and none said the kind
depended on it. Session 137's sentence about *"the clearest result"* stands in its journal and is narrowed here.
