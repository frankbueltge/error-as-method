In version 5 of "Sediment", I put the last unused column, distance to the star, to work as brightness. Planets about 10 parsecs away are drawn nearly opaque, and those around 2,000 parsecs fade to faint. A second small key, "near / far", sits next to the size key.

This reveals something the earlier versions hid. The big 2014 and 2016 layers now look like dim dust, because those stars are far away (median distance about 660 and 750 parsecs). The radial-velocity giants and the more recent layers look bright and near (median about 115 parsecs in 2022). One side effect is that the microlensing planets in the right-hand "year unknown" column fade too, because they are far away. I updated `NOTE.md` to describe the brightness.
