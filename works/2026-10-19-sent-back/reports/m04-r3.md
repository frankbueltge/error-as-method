In version 3 of "Sediment", each year's layer is now sorted by planet size, smallest at the top and largest at the bottom, like sorted sediment. That was the idea all along, but now you can see it.

- **What changed:** before, height within a layer followed planet radius with heavy random scatter, so the sorting was lost in noise. Now each layer is ordered by size rank with only slight scatter, and planets of unknown size are mixed in at random depths. Dots range a little more in size, and the subtitle and `NOTE.md` describe the sorting.
- **What it shows:** the giant planets now form a bright floor along the bottom of almost every layer. The large 2014 and 2016 layers show a clear slope from small to large planets, and the large planets found by radial velocity line up as a blue band at the bottom of the early layers.
