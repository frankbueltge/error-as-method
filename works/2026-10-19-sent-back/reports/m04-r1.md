I made "Sediment", a still canvas image that stacks the 6,451 confirmed exoplanets in layers by discovery year, from 1992 at the top to 2026 at the bottom. Each layer is as thick as the number of planets found that year. Across the page, each planet sits at its orbital period on a log scale running from 2.4 hours to 2,700 years. Dashed lines mark the periods of Mercury, Earth, Jupiter and Neptune. Within a layer, larger planets sit lower, and colour shows how each planet was found. Planets with no known period go in a narrow column on the right.
