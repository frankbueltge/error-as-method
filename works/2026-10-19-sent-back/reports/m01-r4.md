I made a fourth version of "One Sky of Orbits" and looked at it in two new renders (08 and 09). The last one loaded with no page errors.

**What changed:** the dots used to be drawn in discovery order, so the thousands of transit planets could cover the rarer ones. Now they are drawn in layers: transit first, then radial velocity, then the other methods on top with a thin dark edge. In the render, the green "other method" planets and the imaging and microlensing planets now show inside the dense areas instead of being hidden.

**One try I took back:** render 08 also made the transit dots fainter and smaller. That made the largest group, 4,785 planets, look smaller than the 1,204 radial-velocity planets, which misrepresents the data. In render 09 the transit dots are back to roughly their full weight.

**NOTE.md:** updated to explain the layering.

The changes are getting smaller each round. Without a specific direction, another version is unlikely to improve much on this one.
