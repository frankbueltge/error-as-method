I made "Soundings", a still image drawn on one canvas and modelled on a depth sounding. The Sun is a surface line at the top, and each year from 1992 to 2026 is a column. Each of the 6,451 confirmed planets sits in the column for its discovery year, lowered to its star's distance on a log scale from 1 to 10 000 parsecs.

- **Colour** shows the discovery method.
- **Dot size** shows the planet's radius.
- **Unknown distance:** planets without one sit in a strip below the floor.
- **Notes:** four short labels mark 51 Peg b, Proxima Cen b, the Kepler releases of 2014 and 2016, and the deep band of microlensing finds.

I looked at it in the viewer twice. The first render was sound, but two notes needed fixing: the microlensing label pointed at empty space, and the Kepler label sat on top of dots. I moved both and gave the label text a dark outline. The second render has no page errors and reads clearly: early radial-velocity finds stay near the top, Kepler makes two bright, deep columns, and microlensing forms a pink band near the bottom.
