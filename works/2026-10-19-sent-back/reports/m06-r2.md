The new version of "Every Line of Sight" keeps the same dial and adds four labelled landmarks plus a key for dot size. I looked at it: I rendered it three times this round, and the final picture, `renders/06.png`, has no page errors.

**Landmarks.** Each is circled and has a pointer line to a short caption, so the distance scale has some known points:
- PSR B1257+12 c, the first confirmed planet (1992), which orbits a dead pulsar
- 51 Peg b, the first planet found around a Sun-like star (1995)
- Proxima Cen b, the nearest, at 1.3 pc
- SWEEPS-11 b, the farthest with a known distance, at 8,500 pc

**Dot-size key.** It shows dots for half an Earth, Earth, Neptune, Jupiter and twice Jupiter, drawn at double the chart's scale so they can be seen.

**Fixes after looking.** My first try put all the captions in the crowded top of the dial, so I moved them and gave the text a dark outline to keep it readable over the dots. I also corrected one caption that wrongly said SWEEPS-11 b was found by microlensing; it was found by transit.
