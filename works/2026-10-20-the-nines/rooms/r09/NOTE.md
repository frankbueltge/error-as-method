# Eighteen Miles Out

Buoy 46026's year is redrawn as sea, one thin row for each day of 2024, from 1 January at the top to 31 December at the bottom, and from midnight to midnight across. Each row is a drawn wave. Its height follows the significant wave height and its wavelength follows the dominant period, so long winter swells and short, choppy seas leave different textures. Its colour is the water temperature under it, which makes the cold spring upwelling a blue band and the warm late summer a copper one. Where the file holds its 99.00 placeholders the sea is simply left out, so the gap in March shows as an empty strip. A small mark at 4 February shows where the wind and pressure columns turn to nines for good. From then on the buoy reports only water and waves. The page reads `data/buoy.js`, a slot-by-slot copy of the original text file made by `tools/build.js`, because a page opened from `file://` cannot fetch the .txt directly.
