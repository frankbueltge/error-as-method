# Combed Year

Each of the 17,021 half-hourly sea states that buoy 46026 recorded in 2024 is drawn as one stroke. The stroke starts at its moment in the year (left to right) and at its dominant wave period (log scale, bottom to top). It points the way the waves were travelling, with north up, and its length is the significant wave height. Its colour comes from the water temperature at that time, cold slate-blue to warm rust. The buoy measures period in fixed spectral bins, so the strokes fall into ruled rows, like lines in a ledger, and the whole field is combed toward the coast. Winter shows as long, dark, heavy strokes in the long-period rows. Spring upwelling shows as short, cold blue chop. In summer, the long-period rows hold small upright strokes: swell arriving from the far south. The gap in February is where the buoy stopped reporting. The page reads the unchanged text record through `data/46026h2024.js`, which wraps the original file as a JavaScript string so the page can load it without a server.
