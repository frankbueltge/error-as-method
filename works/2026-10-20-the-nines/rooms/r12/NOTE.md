# Swell Ledger

Every day of 2024 at buoy 46026 is one thread, drawn from midnight to midnight and stacked from January at the top to December at the bottom, each day occluding the one above like a ridge of water seen from low on the sea. The thread rises in proportion to the significant wave height, so the winter storms stand up as dark peaks and the summer flattens into tight lines; the spacing of its undulation follows the dominant wave period, long groundswell drawn as slow broad swells and short wind-sea as fine ripple; its colour is the sea-surface temperature, which shows the cold upwelling of May and June and the warm water of September. Where the buoy recorded nothing, as in the late-February to early-March outage, the line stops. One note marks 4 February at 22:50, the buoy's last wind reading, taken in a storm. After that its record is only of the sea. (The page reads the record from `data/46026h2024.js`, a verbatim copy of the .txt wrapped as a JavaScript string, because a page opened from file:// is not allowed to read the plain text file.)
