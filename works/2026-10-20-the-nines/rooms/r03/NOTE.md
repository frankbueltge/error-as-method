# After the Wind

Every ten-minute record of the year becomes one cell of a woven field: the day runs across and the hour runs down, and each cell takes the colour of the sea temperature. The field reads as vertical bands, from the cold upwelling blues of spring to the warm ochres of late summer. Gaps in the record stay blank paper. Under the field, daily wave heights hang down like a fringe. Above it, the wind trace covers only the first five weeks and ends at one point: 4 February, 22:50, 17.8 m/s with gusts to 24.4 and pressure at 989.4 hPa. After that the file holds only the placeholder nines for wind and pressure, and a dotted line marks the silence for the rest of the year while the water goes on being measured. The page reads the unchanged NDBC text (wrapped as a JS string in `data/46026h2024.js` so it loads offline) and draws everything from it.
