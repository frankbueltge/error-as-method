# After the Wind

The year at buoy 46026 is laid out like a recording of sound, one month to a row, with the days of each month lined up. Each wave record becomes one vertical stroke, drawn as far above and below the centre line as the significant wave height. Its colour is the sea temperature at that moment, and longer-period swell is drawn brighter. Across January and early February a thin white line traces the wind. It stops at a single point, 22:50 UTC on 4 February, in the storm, at 17.8 m/s, gusting 24.4. The file holds no wind or pressure after that. The form shows the record losing its sensors one by one: the big winter swells, the cold blue water of spring upwelling, the flat warm days of late summer, the dark gap at the turn of February into March, and a faint thermometer thread where the wave sensor went quiet but the thermometer kept reading.
