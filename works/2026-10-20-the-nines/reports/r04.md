I made "After the Wind", a still canvas image that lays out buoy 46026's year in 12 rows, one per month. It looks something like a sound recording.

- **Waves:** each wave record is one vertical stroke, drawn above and below the centre line by the significant wave height. Its colour is the sea temperature, from cold blue to warm ochre. Longer-period swell is drawn brighter.
- **Wind:** a thin white line traces the wind through January and early February. It ends at a marked point: 4 Feb, 22:50 UTC, during the storm, at 17.8 m/s gusting 24.4 and 989.4 hPa. The file has no wind or pressure readings after that, so the rest of the year shows only the sea.
- **Gaps:** an empty stretch from 26 Feb to 5 Mar is labelled "nothing at all". Where waves are missing but the thermometer was still reading, a faint coloured thread runs along the centre line.

The page couldn't load the raw `.txt` through the viewer: both `fetch` and the fallback request failed, which I took to be the browser blocking local file access. So I added `data/46026h2024.js`, which holds the unchanged text of the record as one string, and `index.html` loads that with a local script tag. Nothing is fetched from the network. The final render is `renders/03.png`; it shows no page errors.
