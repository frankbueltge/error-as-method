I made "Combed Year", a single still canvas image at 1100 × 800. It draws 17,021 strokes, one for each half-hourly sea state that buoy 46026 recorded in 2024:

- **Position:** left to right is the time of year; bottom to top is the dominant wave period, on a log scale.
- **Direction:** each stroke points the way the waves were travelling, with north up.
- **Length:** the significant wave height.
- **Colour:** the water temperature at that time, from cold slate-blue to warm rust.

The buoy measures period in fixed bins, so the strokes fall into ruled rows. Winter shows as long, heavy strokes in the long-period rows. Spring upwelling shows as short, cold chop. In summer, small upright strokes in the long-period rows are swell coming from the far south. The gap in February is where the buoy stopped reporting.

One change from the plan: the viewer could not fetch the text file, so I added `data/46026h2024.js`. It wraps the unchanged record as a JavaScript string so the page loads it from the folder without a server. The page still tries the `.txt` file if the wrapper is missing.

The final render had no page errors.
