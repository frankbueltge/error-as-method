I made "After 22:50", a single still canvas image at 1100×800 in `<scratch>/w/rooms/r11/index.html`. It shows buoy 46026's year as a grid of every ten-minute record. Each column is a day, with midnight at the top, and colour is the sea-surface temperature, the only sensor that reports almost all year.

For January and early February the wind is drawn over the grid as faint strokes pointing the way it blew. The file stops recording wind and pressure at 22:50 UTC on 4 February, during a storm (17.8 m/s from 164°, gusting 24.4, barometer 989.4 hPa), and from then to the end of the year it holds only the placeholder values 99.0 and 9999.0. That last stroke is marked in red with a note under it. After that the picture is just bands of sea temperature through the seasons. Wave height every half hour hangs below the grid, and times with no record at all, such as the gaps in February and March, are left black.

The viewer opens the page straight from disk, and in that mode the browser blocks reading the .txt file. So the page loads `data/46026h2024.js` instead, which I made by wrapping the original text unchanged in a single JavaScript string. The page parses that text itself and uses no network.

The final render is `renders/03.png` and shows no page errors. `NOTE.md` has the title and a paragraph on what the form does.
