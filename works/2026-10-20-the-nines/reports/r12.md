I made "Swell Ledger", a still canvas image of buoy 46026's 2024 record. Each of the 366 days is one thread running from midnight to midnight UTC. The threads are stacked from January at the top to December at the bottom, and each day hides part of the day above it, like ridgelines.

- **Height of the thread:** the significant wave height.
- **Spacing of its waves:** the dominant wave period.
- **Colour:** the sea temperature. The cold upwelling in May and June shows as blue, and the warm water in September as sand-orange.
- **Breaks:** where the record is missing, as in the late-February to early-March outage, the line stops.
- **One note:** it marks 4 February at 22:50. That was the buoy's last wind and pressure reading, taken in a storm (17.8 m/s, gusts 24.4, 989.4 hPa). After that the file reports only the sea.

I had to add one file. The viewer opens the page straight from disk (file://), and a page opened that way is not allowed to read the plain .txt. So the page loads `data/46026h2024.js` instead, which is the .txt copied word for word into a JavaScript string, and all parsing happens in the page. The page renders with no errors, and `NOTE.md` explains this.
