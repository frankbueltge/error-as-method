I made "Eighteen Nautical Miles", a still chart of a voyage by a buoy that is actually moored. The wrong reading I chose is to treat each wave reading as a step. In 2024 there are 17,021 readings with both a wave height and a direction. Each one moves buoy 46026 as many metres as its significant wave height (WVHT), in the direction the waves were travelling (away from MWD). The buoy then crawls east-south-east across a grid of nautical miles toward "San Francisco", placed 18 NM east because the station's name says so. Each reading is also drawn across the track as a hair as long as the wave was tall, so the winter months show as a rough, wide stroke and the summer as a thin one.

The path comes to 33.31 km, which is 17.98 NM and almost exactly the distance to the city. But the waves came from slightly north of west, so the track ends on 31 December 10.3 km short and to the south.

I checked the work in the viewer: the last render, renders/02.png, shows no page errors and no clipped labels.

One addition to the folder: a page opened from disk can't load a local .txt file, so I added `data/46026h2024.js`. It is the raw file unchanged, wrapped in a single JavaScript string, and the page parses it when it loads. NOTE.md explains that the reading is wrong because the buoy is anchored, and because WVHT is a vertical statistic of wave height, not a horizontal distance.
