I made "Eighteen Miles Out", a still canvas image at 1100 × 800. It draws buoy 46026's 2024 as 366 thin rows of drawn sea, one per day, with January at the top and the hours of the day running left to right.

- **Wave height:** sets how tall each row's waves are.
- **Wave period:** sets how long each wave is drawn, so long swells and short choppy seas look different.
- **Colour:** the water temperature under the waves. The cold spring upwelling shows as a blue band and the warm late summer as a copper one.
- **Missing readings:** wherever the file holds its 99.00 placeholders, nothing is drawn, so the gap in March shows as an empty strip.
- **The wind:** the wind and pressure readings stop for good on 4 February, and a small "wind ends" mark in the margin points to that day.

The final render has no page errors.

The viewer opens the page from `file://`, so the browser can't load the .txt file directly. Instead, `tools/build.js` copies the needed columns from the unchanged original into `data/buoy.js`, which `index.html` loads.
