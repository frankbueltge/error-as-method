I made "After the Wind", a still canvas image at 1100×800 in <scratch>/w/rooms/r03/index.html, with the note in <scratch>/w/rooms/r03/NOTE.md. The final render is renders/04.png in that folder.

**What it shows:**
- **The field:** each ten-minute record of 2024 is one cell, with the day running across and the hour running down. Its colour is the sea temperature, from 9.3 °C (dark blue) to 16.8 °C (rust). It reads as vertical bands, cold in spring and warm in late summer.
- **Gaps:** these stay blank, with small rotated labels inside them. There are no records at all from 26 February to 4 March. From 6 to 23 March the sea temperature is missing but the waves were still logged.
- **Waves:** daily wave heights hang below the field as a fringe, dark for the daily mean and pale for the daily highest.
- **Wind:** the wind trace above the field covers only the first five weeks. It ends at one point: 4 February, 22:50, wind 17.8 m/s, gusts to 24.4 and pressure at 989.4 hPa. Then there are 47 hours with no records. When they come back, the file has only the "missing" filler value (all 9s) for wind and pressure for the rest of the year. A dotted line marks that empty stretch.

**Data loading:** the viewer opens the page from local files, so the browser blocked `fetch` of the .txt. To get round this I added `data/46026h2024.js`, which holds the original file's full text, unchanged, as a single JavaScript string. The page reads that file and works out everything from it, with no network use. The original .txt is still there, untouched.
