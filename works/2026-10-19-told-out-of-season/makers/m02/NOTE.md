# Sixty-One Pulses

The picture showed one beat above everything else: once a day the value jumps up and then drains away for the rest of the day. So the work cuts the record into days and stands them side by side. There are 61 columns, one per day, and 24 dots down each column, one per hour counted from the first reading. A dot's size and darkness give the value. Read this way, each day becomes a pulse that is full at the top and thins out below. You can also see the larger story across the page: the pulse is steady for three weeks, falters for two days around day 10, swells once around day 27, then weakens day by day until about day 43, after which the field is almost empty. Under the grid, the same readings run end to end as one line, so the original continuity is not lost. The values were traced back out of a small compressed picture, so they are approximate. The form is meant to show rhythm and change, not exact figures.
