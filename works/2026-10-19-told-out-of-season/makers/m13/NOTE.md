# Every Day, Until

The 1,464 hourly values are folded into a calendar: one thin stroke per hour, one row per week from Monday to Sunday, nine rows from 15 January to 16 March 2025. Each stroke's height and colour (from cold slate to ember) show its value. I chose the fold because the record is mostly one gesture repeated: once a day the value climbs fast and then slowly sinks. Seen as a single long line, that becomes a texture. Stacked as weeks, each day becomes one swell you can count. You can see where the rhythm skips (two quiet stretches, both at the end of a week) and where it stops (the last days of February). After that the rows go nearly still. The shape inside each day is rebuilt from a small, compressed plot at about five hours per pixel. Read the swells as accurate to within a few hours, not to the hour. The weekday columns assume the record starts at midnight on 15 January.
