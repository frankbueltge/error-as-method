# Sixty-One Spans

The record is cut into its 61 days and each day is turned on its side: one horizontal band per day, stacked from first (top) to last (bottom), with the measured value now running from low on the left to high on the right. Each band is made of six thin strips, roughly four hours each, and the ink in them is the ink of the original dots, so a band shows where that day's values actually sat and how densely. I chose this because the plot's real story is not any single value but how wide each day was: in the original, the daily swings blur into a uniform comb, while stacked as spans they become a shape. Broad, wandering days; two brief contractions; one day that reaches farther than any other; then a steady narrowing into a thin thread that drifts to the low side and stays there. Nothing is added that the picture did not hold; it is the same ink, regrouped by day.
