# Sixty-One Fires

The record is a spiral that makes one turn per day and has one segment per hour, 1,464 in all. It starts at the rim on 15 January and winds inward to 16 March. Each segment is coloured on an ember scale from ash to pale gold by how high its value is. The plot showed the same thing every day: a sharp rise at about the same hour, followed by a long slow fall. I put that rise at twelve o'clock on every turn, so each day reads as a flare that burns down as it goes round the clock. Stacked turns show how the days changed: the run of warm rings, a dim stretch in late January, one very bright night on 10 February, and then rings that cool turn by turn into a dark centre where only a faint ripple remains. A spiral keeps the daily rhythm and the two months in one figure, and nothing has to be read off an axis.
