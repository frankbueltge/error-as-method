# Run-out Groove

The 1,464 hourly readings are cut into a single spiral groove, one turn per day of 24 readings. It starts at twelve o'clock on the rim and winds inward over 61 turns. Instead of a height, each reading sets how wide the groove is at that point. Because each turn is exactly one day, things that happen at the same hour every day line up at the same angle. The strong daily rise-and-slow-fall of the first six weeks therefore shows as a dark band in the upper left of the disc and a pale crescent opposite it. The two quiet spells show as pale rings, and the single surge shows as one heavy ring. In the last three weeks the cycle all but stops, and the groove thins to a hairline before it reaches the empty centre. I chose the form of a record because the material came to me as a record: a long trace that is read in one direction. Winding it into a groove keeps the time unbroken and lets the 61 days be stacked on top of each other, so the daily habit and the moment it fades can both be seen at once.
