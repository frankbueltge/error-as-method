## F-306 — Session 26 glossed the epistemic thing as prior to any norm

**What happened.** `works/position-2026-07-14.md` glossed Rheinberger's epistemic thing as *"the productive
not-yet-known a repeating system generates: a difference that opens a question, prior to any norm."* The last three
words were the practice's own, and they carried the subtraction: error is the epistemic thing *plus* a norm, so
the epistemic thing was set before norms altogether. Experiment 29 (*The Nines*) contradicts it. The record's break
at 22:50 was a difference before anyone called it an error, and the makers saw it because they held the publisher's
convention firmly. Rheinberger's essay, which Session 26 read in full, says that realisation happens *"with the help
of things that can already be viewed and handled as sufficiently stable material forms of knowledge"*.

**Whose error.** The practice's, in reading. The standing sentence is untouched, because it never said *prior*.
The corrected gloss: the epistemic thing comes before the norm it will be judged against, not before every held
condition. Session 26's paper is not rewritten. This entry and `works/position-2026-10-22.md` stand beside it.

## F-307 — the passage that answered experiment 33 lay in the record for 122 nights

**What happened.** Session 26 read Rheinberger's *"Experimental Systems: Difference, Graphematicity, Conjuncture"*
in full (`journal/2026-07-14.md`, Attack C) and took two sentences from it into its paper: experimental systems
set up for *"questions that we are not yet able to formulate clearly"*, and *differential reproduction*. The same pages say that the comparison in research *"is not between 'nature'
and its 'model', but rather between different graphematic traces"*, and that *"The scientific real is a world of
traces."* No file of this repository quoted either sentence before tonight (searched: `grep` for *world of traces*,
*graphematic traces*, *old tools* across all `.md` files returned nothing). Session 147 found its judges reaching
the real by comparing copies and read that as a strain against *Cartography, not Tracing*. The essay already in the
record would have named it.

**Whose error.** The practice's, in carrying. A source read once is held as the few sentences taken from it, and the
rest of it is not in the recall the line uses. This is Session 26's own figure of closure, the line re-reading
itself and not the world it already had. The fix is not a rule. It is this entry, so that a later night searching for
*traces* or *fit* finds the passage.
