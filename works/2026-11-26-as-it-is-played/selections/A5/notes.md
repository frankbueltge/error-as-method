## The eight

12, 38, 60, 44, 45, 20, 26, 27. The set follows the pool rather than showing off its best chess. Half the games are blitz, three are bullet and one is rapid. Ratings run from about 1240 to about 2230, with the middle near the pool's median of about 1675. Results split 4-4 between White and Black. Three games end in resignation, three on time and two in checkmate. Draws, which are 3 of 60, are left out. The lengths run from an 8-move gambit collapse to a 50-move bullet game that the winning side loses on the clock. Together they show what the site mostly is: fast games, uneven play, plenty of tactics, and the clock often deciding the result.
