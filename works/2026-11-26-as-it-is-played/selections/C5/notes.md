# Notes

- v1: first pick, chosen for striking finishes plus a few games that only happen online (ultrabullet, a flag loss, a 7-move draw).
- v2: read as a set, 49 and 59 were two 2500+ bullet games, and 59 is a long quiet endgame that shows little on a small board. I swapped 59 for 57 (952 v 935) so that the lowest-rated players, a large part of the site, were in the set.
- v3: v2 had four mates out of eight. In the pool resignation is the most common ending (23 of 60) and mates are about a quarter (16), but only one resigned game was in the set. I swapped 57 for 54 (1173 v 990, rapid, resigned in 13), which keeps a sub-1000 player and is shorter and easier to follow on a small board. I stopped here.

## The eight

08, 56, 49, 07, 01, 22, 47, 54. They cover every speed (ultrabullet, bullet, blitz, rapid) and ratings from 990 to 2605, and they end in every way games end on the site: checkmate (08, 56, 49), time (07, 01), a draw (22) and resignation (47, 54). Each one shows something you can follow on a small board: a mate by promotion, a quick attack on the king, a king hunted across the board by strong bullet players, a long bullet endgame where White makes two queens and Black's clock runs out, a whole 15-second game, a fast repetition between 2500s, a stronger player punishing a weaker one, and the chaotic play typical at the bottom of the rating pool. Most are short, so a viewer can watch all eight side by side.
