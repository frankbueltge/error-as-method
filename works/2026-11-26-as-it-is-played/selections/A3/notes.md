## The eight

The eight are meant to look like the sixty in small, not to be the best games. The time controls are 3 bullet, 4 blitz and 1 rapid, close to the pool's 37/47/15 per cent split. Two games end on time, three by mate and three by resignation. Ratings run from about 1240 to the one elite bullet game near 2700, with the middle of the set around 1600 to 1800 as in the pool. Game lengths cluster around 30 to 45 moves, plus one 7-move game. Five open 1.e4 and three 1.d4, and the wins are split four and four. The set keeps the habits that run through the pool: a winning player who loses on the clock (45), a resignation almost before the game starts (23), clear tactical blunders settled by mate (08, 38), a long grind (30), a flag in a scramble (60), a mismatched rapid pairing (19), and one game at the top of the rating range (59). It drops what the pool has only rarely: draws (3 of 60) and the single 102-move endgame.
