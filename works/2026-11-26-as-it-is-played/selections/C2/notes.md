## The eight

01, 08, 56, 16, 49, 45, 10, 03. Together they cover every speed in the pool (ultrabullet, bullet, blitz, rapid) and ratings from about 920 to 2605, and they include two big rating gaps (10, 16) and top-level bullet (49). The endings vary too. Most are mates, and each of those has a clear final picture: a pawn promoting with mate (08), knight forks (56), rooks on the open file (16) and a king hunted across the board (49). Two games are decided by the clock, the 15-second flag (01) and a completely won position lost on time (45), and one is a draw by perpetual check (03). Each game has an ending a viewer can see at a glance on a small board, and as a set they show what online chess is actually like, not only its prettiest games.
