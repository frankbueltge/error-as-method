# After

1. **Partly.** In speed, how the games ended, rating spread, length, first move and colour of the winner, the eight track the sixty about as closely as eight games can. The sixty have things the eight leave out: three draws (one a 7-move draw between 2400+ players), an ultrabullet game, 30-second bullet, and the very short blitz and bullet games that end in a blunder and a resignation within 10 moves. The one game of 10 moves or fewer in the eight is a rapid game, and time losses are slightly over-represented (3 of 8 against 18 of 60).

2. About 8 of 60 were over before move 15.

3. 16 of 60 ended by checkmate.
