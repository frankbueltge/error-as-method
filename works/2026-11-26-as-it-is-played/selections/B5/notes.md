## The eight

There are four blitz games, three bullet and one rapid; 3 end by resignation, 3 on time and 2 by checkmate. With eight games the draw (3 of 60, so 0.4 of eight) rounds to none, while time losses (2.4) take the extra slot. The openings are 5 with 1.e4, 2 with 1.d4 and one flank opening (1.c4). White wins 4 and Black wins 4. The players run from about 940 to 2250 (median about 1720, pool 1675), and the games run from 10 to 46 moves (median about 29, pool 30). The set includes what online chess looks like rather than what is most instructive: an early-trap resignation in a mismatched rapid game (47), the 2.Qh5 Wayward Queen (28), an Englund Gambit between beginners (57; the gambit appears 5 times in the pool), clock-decided games at blitz and bullet (60, 53, 29), and two mating attacks, one at ~950 and one at ~2240 (57, 36).
