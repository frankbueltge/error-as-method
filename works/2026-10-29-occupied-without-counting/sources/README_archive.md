The analysis plans of E1–E4 were written before data collection but were not deposited in a public registry; the article accordingly describes those analyses as planned before data collection rather than as pre-registered. The E5 plan (`analysis_plan_E5.md`) was likewise fixed, with its two amendments, before the rating data of E5 were collected, and was likewise not deposited in a registry; the article describes E5 as planned before data collection. Section 11 of the plan records corrections found in an audit after analysis (norming counts, the power-simulation error, convergence diagnostics, the bootstrap lower bound, list assignment). The filler and attention items shown with each experiment are in `fillers_E1E2.csv` and `fillers_E3.csv`. Ratings were collected on Prolific in October 2026 on a seven-point naturalness scale. Participant identifiers have been replaced by pseudonyms; no demographic fields are included except the flag `excl_d`, which marks the participants excluded under criterion (d) of the plans (self-reported country outside the US/UK: three in E3, two in E4, none in E1, five in E5). The scripts reproduce the statistics in footnotes 6 and 8 of the article when run on the data files.

**Version 6** (audit corrections, 2026-10-13): `power_sim_E5.R` corrected (the original kept as `power_sim_E5_original.R` with its output); `analysis_E5.R` now prints and requires convergence diagnostics (optimizer code, maximum gradient, Hessian condition number) for the primary model ladder, with the rerun output; `analysis_E4.R` adds an exploratory split of the follow-up penalties by item origin (16 items reused from E1 vs 32 new); `sensitivity_random_slopes.R` now excludes practice trials before z-scoring, as the other scripts do (output rerun; the maximal by-item t for E3 is 2.12); `norming_check_E5.py` reads the archived `rater` column and reports both-version passes; `bootstrap_participants_E5.py` added; section 11 added to `analysis_plan_E5.md`. Lists were assigned at random by the experiment page in all studies.

**Version 5** adds Experiment 5, the replication of E3 with 32 new items (materials in both versions, fillers, the analysis plan with its amendments, the two norming rounds, trial-level data, analysis script and output, power simulation). Ratings for E5 were collected on Prolific on 12 October 2026.

**Version 4** adds `power_sim_E4.R` and `power_sim_E4_output.txt`, a documented re-implementation of the pre-data power simulation for E4 (500 runs; the parameters are those stated in `analysis_plan_E4.md`), and `satterthwaite_df.R` with its output, which supplies the degrees of freedom and p-values of the linear-model t statistics. The maximal by-item model for E3 does not converge; its t statistic is 2.07 in `sensitivity_output.txt` (lme4) and 2.12 in `satterthwaite_df_output.txt` (lmerTest refit), and footnote 6 of the article reports only the converged by-item and by-participant models; the fuller structures are documented here.

**Version 3** adds the by-participant random-slope sensitivity analyses (`sens_participant_slopes.R`).

**Version 2** adds E4 (materials, fillers, plan, data, script, output), the sensitivity analyses, and the `excl_d` flag; the E3 statistics were recomputed with criterion (d) applied.
