I made "Liquid Range", a single still image drawn on a canvas in <root>/rooms/c1/index.html. All 118 elements stand side by side, hydrogen to oganesson, as columns on a logarithmic temperature scale. Each column is grey stone up to the element's melting point. From there to its boiling point it is the element's CPK colour, and above that there is only a faint thread of vapour. Together the columns read as a landscape: the cold gases sit low, the transition metals form a high ridge, and only mercury and bromine cross the dotted "room, 20 °C" line. Arsenic, which goes straight from solid to gas, shows as a thin seam instead of a band. Elements with no boiling point fade upward, and the superheavy elements with no data at all have ground that thins out into the dark.

The page reads everything from `ELEMENTS` when it runs, loads nothing outside the folder and needed no libraries. The viewer rendered it with no page errors; the final render is <root>/rooms/c1/renders/02.png. The note is in <root>/rooms/c1/NOTE.md.
