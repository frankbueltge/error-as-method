I made "The Liquid Interval". It is a single still canvas image at 1100 × 800, in `<root>/rooms/c4/index.html`.

- **Layout:** each of the 118 elements is one column, in order of atomic number. The vertical axis is temperature in kelvin on a log scale, with its range worked out from the data.
- **Solid:** a faint hairline rises from the floor up to the melting point.
- **Liquid:** a bar in the element's CPK colour runs from melting point to boiling point.
- **Gas:** sparse points continue above the boiling point.
- **Missing values:** where no boiling point is recorded, the bar fades upward with no end. The last 15 elements have neither melting nor boiling point and are left as empty dotted columns.
- **Room temperature:** a line labelled "this room" marks it. Only bromine and mercury cross it, and they are named on the image.

The page reads everything from `ELEMENTS` at runtime and loads nothing from outside the folder. I rendered it once with the viewer (`renders/01.png`), and it loaded with no page errors. `NOTE.md` is next to it, in the same folder.
