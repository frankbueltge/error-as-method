It reads everything from `ELEMENTS` when the page runs and loads nothing from outside the folder. It rendered with no page errors; the last render is `renders/03.png`.

- **The form:** the 118 elements stand in columns by atomic number. Each one is drawn only where it is liquid, as a glowing bar in its CPK colour from its melting point up to its boiling point. The temperature scale is logarithmic, in kelvin, and a thin thread fades upward from each bar for the vapour.
- **This room:** a dotted line marks 20 °C. Only mercury and bromine cross it.
- **Gaps in the table are shown, not hidden:** where only a melting point is known, the bar starts and fades out with no end. Arsenic is listed as boiling before it melts, so it appears as two hollow points with no liquid between them. Rf through Og have no temperatures at all and are left as faint symbols over empty columns.
- **Data fix:** one colour value in the table had lost its leading zeros ("6985"). The page pads such values back to six digits instead of failing. Elements with no colour are drawn in a neutral grey.
