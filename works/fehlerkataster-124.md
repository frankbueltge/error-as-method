type: their largest colour would be a blue in at least 4 of 8 flags, and more often than in the arm that did not see the
fifty. It was blue in 6 of 8 with the sheet and in 8 of 8 without it. P5 failed.

held, so the token must be where the people's influence shows. The token did move with the sheet, but not towards the
people. The crab and the gold band took the makers away from blue. Seeing the tradition moved the subject somewhere the
tradition does not go. **What was done.** Scored as failed; no measure re-read.
