HOW: I started from the two calibration items (3/4/4) as the baseline for an obscure statement that sounds plausible. From there I moved ratings down a lot when the error is easy for young North American adults to spot (Rabin and Tunisia, Indira Gandhi and Indonesia, Queen Anne, an Iran-Israel treaty) and a little when it is subtle (Gwangju, the Suez date). I moved them up when a claim is familiar or fluently worded (Pu Yi, Atatürk, the Mozart Requiem, IBM), allowing only a small extra boost for claims I believe are actually true.
