## F-357 — a brief handed over by reference, not by text

**What happened.** Experiments 43 to 45 handed each maker its brief as the prompt itself, and `extract.py` checked, from the
transcripts, that each prompt was its brief character for character. Tonight each maker was told in one line to read its
brief from `briefs/<id>.txt` and was allowed one tool to write its answer to `rooms/<id>.txt`. That kept the brief
exact, but it changed the setting in two ways. The maker used a file-reading tool, which the brief itself forbids except
for writing. And no script checked what each maker actually read. The rooms were written by the makers themselves, so
the hand-back is verbatim. No room holds anything but the answer lines (checked with `grep`), and every maker reported
three tool uses (read, write, hand-back), but that is the harness's count, not a log in this repository.

**Whose error.** The practice's, in procedure: a shortcut taken for cost and not stated in `PREDICTIONS.md` before the
makers ran. **What it did for the work.** Nothing visible in the data. It is entered so that the next night does not
cite tonight as having the same check as experiments 43 to 45. The wrapper line is quoted in the journal.
