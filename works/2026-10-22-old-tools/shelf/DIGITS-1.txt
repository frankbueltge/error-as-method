1. US Census Bureau — County and city population estimates (resident population of every US county and incorporated place)
2. GeoNames — allCountries / cities1000 gazetteer (population and elevation of named places worldwide)
3. World Bank — World Development Indicators (GDP, population, and other national figures per country and year)
4. US Federal Election Commission — Individual contributions bulk data (amount of each itemized donation to federal campaigns)
5. USAspending.gov — Federal award transactions (dollar amount of every federal contract and grant obligation)
6. UK HM Treasury / government departments — Spending over £25,000 (amount of each departmental payment to suppliers)
7. City of Chicago Data Portal — Employee salaries (annual salary or hourly rate of every city employee)
8. SEC EDGAR — Financial Statement Data Sets (line-item values reported in company 10-K and 10-Q filings)
9. Natural Earth — Admin-0 and Admin-1 boundaries (land area and population attributes of countries and provinces)
10. OpenStreetMap / Geofabrik — Building and road extracts (lengths and areas of mapped features)
11. GitHub Archive / Software Heritage — Repository metadata (file sizes, star counts, and commit counts of public repositories)
12. Kaggle mirror of Steam / SteamSpy — Game catalogue (owner counts, prices, and playtime of games on Steam)
13. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — Trip record data (fare amount, trip distance, and tip of every yellow-cab trip)
14. US Bureau of Transportation Statistics — Airline On-Time Performance (departure delays and flight distances of domestic flights)
15. Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF) — Species occurrence counts (number of recorded observations per species)
16. NASA JPL Small-Body Database — Asteroid and comet orbital elements (diameter, semi-major axis, and orbital period of minor bodies)
17. Hipparcos / Gaia DR3 (ESA) — Stellar catalogue (parallax-derived distances and luminosities of stars)
18. UN Comtrade — International merchandise trade (value in US dollars of goods traded between pairs of countries)
19. Eurostat — NUTS 3 regional statistics (population, GDP, and area of European regions)
20. Project Gutenberg — Catalogue and texts (word counts and file sizes of public-domain books)
