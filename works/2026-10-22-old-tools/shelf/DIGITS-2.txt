1. U.S. Census Bureau — County and city population estimates (resident population of every U.S. county and incorporated place, by year)
2. World Bank — World Development Indicators (GDP, population, land area and hundreds of other national figures per country and year)
3. USGS / GNIS — Geographic Names Information System (named lakes, summits and streams, with elevations and areas)
4. NASA JPL — Small-Body Database (orbital elements, diameters and absolute magnitudes of asteroids and comets)
5. GBIF — Species occurrence downloads (individual counts reported in biodiversity observations)
6. U.S. Federal Election Commission — Individual contributions file (the amount of each itemized donation to federal campaigns)
7. USAspending.gov — Federal contract and grant awards (dollar value of each award)
8. Companies House (UK) — Accounts bulk data (reported balance-sheet figures of registered companies)
9. U.S. SEC — Financial Statement Data Sets (numeric values from XBRL filings of listed companies)
10. Nasdaq / Yahoo Finance historical data — Daily trading volume of listed stocks (shares traded per day)
11. UN Comtrade — International merchandise trade (export and import values by country pair and commodity)
12. FAOSTAT — Crop production (tonnes harvested and area harvested per crop, country and year)
13. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — Trip record data (fare amounts and trip distances of each ride)
14. City of Chicago Data Portal — Employee salaries (annual salary of each city employee)
15. Global Power Plant Database (WRI) — Power plants of the world (installed capacity in MW of each plant)
16. OpenStreetMap / Natural Earth — Populated places (population figures attached to cities and towns)
17. Software Heritage / Debian — Package archive metadata (installed sizes in bytes of each Debian package)
18. The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Open Access collection (object dimensions and accession data of artworks)
19. IMDb — Non-commercial datasets (number of votes received by each title)
20. Global Biodiversity / IUCN-adjacent: Lake and reservoir HydroLAKES (HydroSHEDS) — Surface area and volume of every mapped lake on Earth
