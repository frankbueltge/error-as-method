1. NOAA NCEI — Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (daily air temperature and precipitation readings from weather stations, some going back to the 1800s)
2. European Central Bank — Euro foreign exchange reference rates (daily rates published on TARGET working days since 1999)
3. US Bureau of Labor Statistics — Consumer Price Index, CPI-U series (monthly price index, including months missed during government shutdowns)
4. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, FRED — 10-Year Treasury Constant Maturity Rate, DGS10 (daily yields on business days)
5. NOAA National Hurricane Center — HURDAT2 Atlantic best track (6-hourly positions and intensities of tropical cyclones, with extra fixes added at landfall)
6. Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program — Volcanoes of the World eruption database (start and end dates of Holocene eruptions)
7. Met Office Hadley Centre — HadCET Central England Temperature (daily mean temperature since 1772, monthly since 1659)
8. Old Bailey Proceedings Online — trial records 1674–1913 (dates of court sessions and the trials held in them)
9. Voteview (UCLA) — US Congress roll-call votes (date and time of every recorded vote in the House and Senate)
10. Federal Register / GovInfo — Federal Register daily issues (publication date of every issue and document)
11. Project Gutenberg — catalog metadata (release dates by ebook number)
12. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX API (capture timestamps for a single URL)
13. Linux kernel (kernel.org) — git commit history (author and commit timestamps of every commit)
14. Python Software Foundation — CPython release history (release date of every version)
15. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — Trip Record Data (pickup and dropoff timestamps of every yellow cab trip)
16. Billboard / public Hot 100 archives on GitHub — weekly Hot 100 charts since 1958 (chart dates and positions)
17. Hacker News — official Firebase API / BigQuery public dataset (creation time of every story and comment)
18. Supreme Court Database (Washington University) — case-centered data (decision date of every US Supreme Court case since 1791)
19. OpenSky Network — historical state vectors (timestamped aircraft position reports)
20. NASA PDS / JPL — Mars Science Laboratory, Curiosity raw image index (acquisition time of every image, by sol)
