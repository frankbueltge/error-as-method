1. Met Office Hadley Centre — Central England Temperature series (daily and monthly mean air temperature in central England since 1659/1772)
2. NASA GISS — GISTEMP (global mean surface temperature anomaly by month since 1880)
3. NSIDC — Global Lake and River Ice Phenology database (freeze and breakup dates, ice duration of lakes such as Lake Mendota)
4. Smithsonian Institution Global Volcanism Program — Holocene eruption database (eruption dates, Volcanic Explosivity Index)
5. NOAA National Hurricane Center — HURDAT2 Atlantic hurricane database (6-hourly position, maximum wind, central pressure of tropical cyclones)
6. NASA Ozone Watch — Antarctic ozone hole data (yearly minimum total ozone and ozone-hole area)
7. NOAA NCEI Paleoclimatology — Vostok ice core deuterium record (reconstructed Antarctic temperature over 400,000 years)
8. Hudson's Bay Company records via Elton & Nicholson (1942) — Canadian lynx pelt returns (lynx trapped per year, 1821–1934)
9. USDA NASS — Honey Bee Colonies report (number of bee colonies and colony losses by quarter)
10. FAO — FishStat global capture production (tonnes of fish caught per year)
11. Bank of England — A Millennium of Macroeconomic Data (Bank Rate, prices, wages in the UK since the 13th century)
12. Robert Shiller, Yale — U.S. stock market data (monthly S&P composite price, dividends, earnings, CPI since 1871)
13. U.S. Energy Information Administration — Weekly retail gasoline prices (US average price per gallon since 1990)
14. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED — UNRATE (monthly US civilian unemployment rate since 1948)
15. Human Mortality Database — Deaths and life tables (deaths and death rates by year and age for about 40 countries)
16. U.S. Transportation Security Administration — Checkpoint travel numbers (daily count of passengers screened at US airports)
17. Old Bailey Proceedings Online — Trials at London's Central Criminal Court 1674–1913 (number of trials, verdicts, punishments per year)
18. Google Books Ngram Viewer — Ngram datasets (yearly relative frequency of a word in printed books)
19. Sean Lahman — Lahman Baseball Database (season statistics such as runs, home runs, batting average per team and player since 1871)
20. Boston Athletic Association — Boston Marathon results (winning times by year since 1897)
