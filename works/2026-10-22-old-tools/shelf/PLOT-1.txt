1. NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies — GISTEMP v4 (global mean surface air and sea temperature anomaly, monthly since 1880)
2. NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory — Global monthly mean methane (CH4 concentration in the atmosphere, since 1983)
3. NOAA Climate Prediction Center — Oceanic Niño Index (3-month running sea surface temperature anomaly in the Niño 3.4 region, since 1950)
4. Met Office Hadley Centre — HadCET Central England Temperature (daily and monthly mean air temperature, monthly since 1659)
5. Lisiecki & Raymo (PANGAEA / NOAA Paleoclimatology) — LR04 benthic δ18O stack (global ice volume and deep-ocean temperature proxy over 5.3 million years)
6. NOAA Paleoclimatology — Vostok ice core deuterium record (reconstructed Antarctic temperature over about 420,000 years)
7. NOAA National Hurricane Center — HURDAT2 Atlantic hurricane database (six-hourly position, maximum wind and pressure of every tropical cyclone since 1851)
8. U.S. Energy Information Administration — Weekly U.S. retail gasoline prices (average price per gallon of regular gasoline, since 1990)
9. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) — UNRATE (monthly U.S. civilian unemployment rate, since 1948)
10. Robert Shiller, Yale — U.S. stock market data (monthly S&P composite price, dividends, earnings and CAPE ratio, since 1871)
11. European Central Bank — Euro foreign exchange reference rates (daily USD per EUR, since 1999)
12. Bank of England — A millennium of macroeconomic data (UK prices, wages, interest rates and output, annual from the 13th century)
13. U.S. Census Bureau — New Residential Construction (monthly housing starts, since 1959)
14. U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics — T-100 / Air Carrier Traffic Statistics (monthly passengers carried by U.S. airlines)
15. U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — FluView ILINet (weekly percentage of doctor visits for influenza-like illness, since 1997)
16. Office for National Statistics — Weekly deaths registered in England and Wales (deaths per week, since 2010)
17. Human Mortality Database — Period life tables (life expectancy at birth per year, by country)
18. U.S. Social Security Administration — Baby names from Social Security card applications (yearly count of babies given each first name, since 1880)
19. USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service — Quick Stats corn yield (U.S. bushels of corn per acre, yearly since 1866)
20. NYC Open Data — 311 Service Requests (every non-emergency complaint to New York City, since 2010, countable per day)
