1. NOAA National Hurricane Center — HURDAT2 Atlantic best track (six-hourly positions and intensities of every tropical cyclone, with extra off-cadence landfall entries)
2. Met Office Hadley Centre — HadCET Central England Temperature daily series (daily mean temperature in central England since 1772)
3. NOAA NCEI — Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (daily temperature and precipitation at individual land weather stations)
4. European Central Bank — Euro foreign exchange reference rates (daily euro rates on TARGET business days since 1999)
5. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) — DGS10 10-year Treasury constant maturity yield (daily US government bond yields on market days)
6. US Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI-U monthly series CUUR0000SA0 (monthly consumer price index, including months skipped during government shutdowns)
7. US Treasury Fiscal Data — Daily Treasury Statement (daily federal government cash and debt operations)
8. Federal Register (federalregister.gov API) — Federal Register documents by publication date (daily issues of US federal rules and notices)
9. Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program — Holocene eruption database (start dates of known volcanic eruptions worldwide)
10. Jonathan McDowell — GCAT General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects, launch list (dates and times of every orbital launch attempt)
11. UK Parliament — Hansard sitting days (dates on which the House of Commons sat and debates were recorded)
12. Voteview (UCLA) — US Congress roll call votes (date and result of every recorded vote in the House and Senate)
13. Washington University Law — Supreme Court Database (decision dates of US Supreme Court cases since 1791)
14. Old Bailey Proceedings Online — sessions papers (dates of trial sessions at London's Central Criminal Court, 1674–1913)
15. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX API (capture timestamps of every archived snapshot of a given URL)
16. Linux kernel (kernel.org git) — commit log of torvalds/linux (author and commit timestamps of every change to the kernel)
17. Stack Exchange — public data dump, Posts table (creation timestamps of every question and answer on a site)
18. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — TLC trip record data (pickup and dropoff timestamps of individual taxi trips)
19. City of Chicago Data Portal — Crimes 2001 to present (date and time of every reported crime incident)
20. Billboard via Kaggle / data.world — Hot 100 weekly charts (chart dates and rankings of the weekly US singles chart since 1958)
