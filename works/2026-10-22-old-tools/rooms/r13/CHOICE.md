# First to mind

1. NYC 311 service requests (noise complaints), counted hour by hour
2. Hourly pedestrian counts from the City of Melbourne sensors
3. Daily hospital emergency department visits

# Chosen

NYC 311 Service Requests from 2010 to Present (NYC Open Data, City of New York), restricted to the
complaint type "Noise - Residential" and counted per hour from Monday 5 May 2025 to Sunday 19 October
2025 (24 whole weeks, 4032 hours). URL: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Social-Services/311-Service-Requests-from-2010-to-Present/erm2-nwe9
(fetched through its Socrata API, https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/erm2-nwe9.csv).
How I came to it: it was the first thing to mind, it is outside every domain already used, and it is a
record of people's patience with their neighbours, which should beat to the clock and the calendar
at once. I chose a window between the two daylight-saving changes so the hours stay even.

# Expected

I expect the strongest period to be 24 hours (complaints peak late at night), with the weekly
period of 168 hours second, because weekend nights (parties) should be much louder than weeknights;
a 12-hour harmonic may come third.
