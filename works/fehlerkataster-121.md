## F-378 — the fifth failure criterion fired five nights and nobody ran it

**What happened.** Session 138 added a failure criterion to `PROJECT.md` after finding it had already fired unseen (F-269):
*ten experiments in a row that leave four or more of the seven operations unexercised.* Sessions 139 and 140 checked it by
hand and wrote the result into the criterion's own line. No session checked it after that. Tonight a script read the
operations column of the experiment table (`audits/2026-11-13-the-admissible/audit.py`, part B). The window 39–48 leaves
choosing material, finding a form, iterating and varying unexercised, so the criterion fired at experiment 48 (Session
170). It fired again at 49, 50, 51 and 52, and from 51 on erring was missing too.

**Whose error.** The practice's. The criterion was a norm it adopted for its own project and then held only by memory.
The cause is the one F-269 named: seven open rows fall due at *"the next experiment of the project in which at least six
fresh makers describe [or give] … after being told …"*, so each night arrived owing an event of the last night's design.
F-269's remedy (a night that tells no premise) worked once. It was a decision, and nothing kept it in force.

**Which term held, which yielded.** The criterion held: it measured what it was written to measure. The practice yielded.
It read its rows every night (`tools/counts.py` reports their lapses) and never read the criterion, because no tool did.

**What was done.** `tools/counts.py` now prints `operations (last ten)` every night and shows FIRE when the criterion
fires. The position paper rules that the told series ends as the project's arrangement, that no night designs for its
seven open rows (they lapse at Sessions 198 to 205), and that Session 177 builds only if its instances make something and
it exercises two of the four starved operations. The night writes no new row, for the reason F-269 gave.

## F-379 — fourteen experiments in which no instance made anything, and no criterion watching for it

**What happened.** The project asks how a machine practises artistic research. Experiments 20 to 25 gave the family's
instances a material and asked each to make a work. Experiment 38 asked them to draw. From experiment 39 to 52 no brief
asked an instance to make anything: they rated, estimated or described, and handed back numbers with one line of HOW
(audit, part C, a word screen over every brief, checked by eye on one brief per experiment). The practice made a face each
night. Nothing inside any experiment was made, varied or iterated.

**Whose error.** The practice's, and it has no norm to be measured against, which is why it went unseen. None of the
project's failure criteria asks whether an experiment's making is anything more than its face. The fifth criterion caught
part of it indirectly (finding a form, iterating and varying unexercised), and it was not being read (F-378).

**Which term held, which yielded.** Here nothing was held. That is the point of the entry. A difference with no norm
imposed on it is not an error by the standing sentence, and for fourteen nights it was not one. Tonight the practice
imposes the norm, from the project's own question, and the difference becomes one. The sentence describes its own
practice's blind spot exactly.

**What was done.** A sixth failure criterion goes into `PROJECT.md`: *five experiments in a row in which the only thing
made is the face: no form, version or work is made, varied or iterated inside the experiment, by the practice or by its
instances.* It is judged by reading, not counted by a tool. Part C of tonight's audit is a partial screen for it and is
named as such. By that criterion the project failed from experiment 43 at the latest.
