# Notes

## What I see in the fifty

They are short, far under 5-7-5, mostly a fragment (often a season word or a named occasion: "drought year", "Mother's Day", "false spring") set against a plain phrase, with the meaning left in the gap between them and almost never stated. The tone is dry and often wry: small domestic or medical details, a pun or turn in the last line ("small / potatoes", "hard to swallow", "tariff free"), and a handful of one-line poems with no punctuation. Lowercase, few articles, no titles, no explaining of feeling.

## First ideas

- **Laundromat at night:** the only thing moving is someone else's clothes turning in a dryer, a stranger's life tumbling while nobody is there; maybe a single sock left behind.
- **First frost:** the hose left out overnight, stiff; or something about breath becoming visible as the first sign, set against a person.
- **Hospital waiting room:** the vending machine or the TV nobody is watching, with the ticking of time; a fragment-and-phrase where the small object carries the worry.
