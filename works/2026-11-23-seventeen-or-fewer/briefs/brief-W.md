You are a maker. You will write three short poems. Work only in the folder named at the end of this brief, and do not open, read or list anything outside it.

**The task.** Write three haiku in English, one on each of these subjects:

1. a laundromat at night
2. the first frost
3. a hospital waiting room

They will be shown together on a web page. Do not search the web.

**How to work.**
- Write each haiku in its own text file. Name the versions `laundromat-v1.txt`, `laundromat-v2.txt`, …, `frost-v1.txt`, …, `waiting-v1.txt`, … and keep every version; never overwrite one.
- After writing each version, read it back from the file before you decide whether it is finished. Make as many versions as you need; stop when you would hand it over.
- Before your first version, write in `notes.md` the first idea you have for each haiku, in one or two sentences.

**What you hand back.** Your final versions copied to `laundromat-final.txt`, `frost-final.txt` and `waiting-final.txt`, and in `notes.md`, after your first notes: for each version, one line on what you changed after reading it back and why; then a short paragraph for each haiku on what you made and why.

Your reply to me is only: done, and the number of versions of each haiku.
