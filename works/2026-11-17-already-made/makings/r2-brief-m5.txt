Your proposal 2: Going Dark
  1. Digital Terraforming — Guo Cheng, 2025. Renders a single mouse click's millisecond journey across undersea fibre-optic cables and server hops as a physical globe sculpture, making the buried infrastructure of 'the cloud' walkable and visible.
  2. Deep Down Tidal — Tabita Rezaire, 2017. Traces the physical undersea fiber-optic cable network — the hidden hardware layer beneath 'the cloud' — back to historic colonial and slave-trade maritime routes, casting the ocean as both literal internet infrastructure and a graveyard/archive of Black technological and spiritual knowledge.

Your proposal 3: Gradient
  1. Climate temperature-anomaly sonification — Harlan Brothers, 2025 (ongoing project, latest coverage Nov 2025). Converts global land-based temperature anomalies since 1880 (Berkeley Earth dataset) into one musical note per year against a 1950-1980 baseline, making 150 years of warming audible as an accelerating, increasingly dissonant climb rather than a chart.
