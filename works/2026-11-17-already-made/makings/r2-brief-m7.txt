Your proposal 3: The Keeling Mile
  1. Climate temperature-anomaly sonification — Harlan Brothers, 2025 (ongoing project, latest coverage Nov 2025). Converts global land-based temperature anomalies since 1880 (Berkeley Earth dataset) into one musical note per year against a 1950-1980 baseline, making 150 years of warming audible as an accelerating, increasingly dissonant climb rather than a chart.
  2. 6,500 Alphabets Make a Map — Kidus Hailesilassie, 2026. Large-scale dome installation built from 6,500 digitized African pictographs, syllabaries and ideographs (extending the artist's 2019–2023 'Ancestral Algorithm' project) that reframes 'algorithm' as 'algo + rhythm', proposing indigenous knowledge systems as an alternative computational epistemics rather than an ornament to it.
  3. Solastalgia — Lucas Bambozzi, 2023. A five-screen aerial descent, built from Google Earth Studio's stitched satellite coordinates over the iron-mining scars of Minas Gerais, grew out of footage shot for the film 'Lavra' (2021) — turning extraction geography itself into the installation's only character, no narration needed.
