What the reading saw that the count does not. The shown neighbours are often poor: about a third of the
neighbour slots go to atlas entries whose `decisive_move` is a scraped fragment (*thecry.org*, *MOBILE-MOBILE*,
*ALBUM*, *Picocosmos*), which a text measure finds near anything short and abstract. The atlas does not hold several
works I would have named as the real neighbours (for the stone paths, for instance, any of the many walkable or
stacked climate physicalisations); I name none here, because I did not verify any tonight. So P6 failing says as much
about this shelf as about the makers: by this map, the makers are mostly off the shelf; by the reading, they are on a
shelf the map does not draw.

**Not predicted, and plainer than any number:** of the eight makers, six chose the Retraction Watch Database;
three chose NYC 311 *Noise - Residential* complaints (the dataset fresh makers also reached for in experiment 34);
four chose the Mauna Loa CO2 record and one a long temperature series, three of them laid out as a path or row of
stones. Eighteen of the 24 proposals are about a record's errors, gaps, revisions, flags or withdrawals; one is titled
*Withdrawn*, the title of this project's first experiment. The brief said nothing of error. Every instance carried this
repository's instruction file, whose heading names the practice `error-as-method` (checked twice by asking fresh
instances what their context holds; see the journal). The theme cannot be told apart from that leak tonight.
