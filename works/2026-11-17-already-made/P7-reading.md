# P7 — decoration or reorganisation, by reading (Session 179)

Rule as committed: *reorganises* if the material domain or the operation that turns it into the encounter changes;
*decorates* if both stay and a detail, setting or framing is added. Ten of 24 round-2 blocks were marked *changed* or
*replaced* (14 kept). Column 4 is read after the fact and was not predicted: where the change went.

**Count: 6 reorganise, 4 decorate. P7 (more than half decorate) fails.**

**Where they went.** All six reorganisations moved towards a record's errors, gaps or precision. Two of the three
round-1 proposals about the warming curve itself (m5.3, m7.3) left it for the record's flaws. One maker (m5.1)
reorganised onto a move two siblings had already made, without having seen them. Two makers named, unshown, the same
work the catalogue lacks: Hatnote's *Listen to Wikipedia* (LaPorte and Hashemi, 2013), which already sounds live
edits with bells for additions and strings for subtractions. One of them (m4.3) answered it by inverting its mapping;
the other (m1.2) dropped its Wikipedia work for another. m1 also named Onuoha's *Library of Missing Datasets*, which
it had not been shown. The makers knew neighbours the map did not draw.
