I made "The Long Wait", a still canvas image at 1100 × 800 in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/12d6a705-ad2d-53d3-8b3a-28a1dba3b3ff/scratchpad/s140/rooms/r15/index.html. It draws all 1,035 laureates who have a birth or founding year as thin horizontal threads. Each thread runs from that year to the year of the prize, and the threads are stacked in award order. The right edge forms a staircase that is the calendar, and the gap to the left edge is the life lived before the prize.

Men are pale, the 70 women are vermilion and organisations are blue. A few labels pick out Passy, Marie Curie, Goodenough, Malala and the missing years 1940–42, and a closing line gives the median age at the prize: 55 before 1950 and 67 since 1990.

I looked at it three times with the viewer and fixed the problems I saw: the title block overlapped the threads, the labels collided, and one year label was cut off at the edge.
