The problem with v2 was that its log money axis drew one purse curve four times. The share only moved each copy up or down by a fixed amount, so you could see how often prizes were split only by comparing pile thickness across the four bands. v3 replaces that with one stacked field where area equals money:
- **Width:** each year is a column as wide as that year's purse in today's kronor, so the time axis is the purse laid end to end.
- **Height:** each laureate is one piece whose height is their share of a prize, so its area is what they took home. Whole prizes sit at the foot of each column, with halves, thirds and quarters above.
- **Colour:** pieces are coloured by man, woman or organisation.

The purse's fall and recovery now shows once, in how wide each decade is. The 1940s are 24 M kr wide and the 2000s 146 M kr. More and more split prizes now show as the tops of the columns breaking into thin pieces. Years with no prize take no width.

I took out the log axis, the four echo lines and their labels, the pile-thickness legend, the evenly spaced calendar axis and the dots that marked empty years. I added a 10 M kr scale bar, brackets for the 1919, 1940s and 2000s widths, and short notes on 2001 and on Economics starting in 1969.
