I made v2 and checked it with the viewer four times; the last render is renders/04.png.

**What changed:** v1's six category strips are now one field. Each laureate is a short dash. Across the page is the year; up the page is the money that laureate took home, which is the prize in today's kronor times their share, on a log scale. The colours still mean man, woman or organisation.

**Why:** every category gets the same purse in a given year, so v1 drew the same outline six times. In the new version that sameness does the work: the dashes fall into four copies of one purse curve, for a whole prize, a half, a third and a quarter. Faint lines trace the four curves. That makes the shares a scale you can read directly, and how thick a pile is shows how many laureates took that share in a year.

**What I removed, and the trade-off:** the category strips, their counts and the stacked columns are gone. Category is no longer shown at all. That was deliberate, because it doesn't change the money, but you can no longer see which categories split their prizes most.

**Facts checked against the data:** I corrected one claim from v1's footer. The purse hit its low in 1919 at 3.0 M kr, under a third of the 1901 value of 10.9 M kr, rather than "by the 1920s". It recovered around 1990.
