I made "The Long Wait", a still canvas piece in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/12d6a705-ad2d-53d3-8b3a-28a1dba3b3ff/scratchpad/s140/rooms/r18/index.html`. Each of the 1,036 awards is a semicircle above a timeline running from 1815 to 2030. The arc starts at the laureate's year of birth (or an organisation's founding) and lands in the year of the prize, so its width is the time before the prize. Men are faint ink, women red and organisations blue. Below the line there is one mark per laureate in each prize year, so the years with no prizes (1940–42) show as a gap.

A few cases are labelled:
- **John B. Goodenough:** the widest span, 97 years.
- **Malala Yousafzai:** the narrowest arc, at 17.
- **The Red Cross:** three arcs from 1863.
- **The Friends Service Council:** its arc from 1647 is too wide for the page and runs off the top.
