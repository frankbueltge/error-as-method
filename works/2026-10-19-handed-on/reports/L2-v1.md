I made "The Long Wait", a single still canvas image in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/12d6a705-ad2d-53d3-8b3a-28a1dba3b3ff/scratchpad/s140/rooms/r16/index.html`. Each laureate is one horizontal line that runs from the year they were born (or founded) to the year of their prize. The lines are stacked in award order, from 1901 at the top to 2026 at the bottom, so the length of each line is the laureate's age at the prize.

- **The overall shape:** the prize years form a sharp staircase down the right edge, and the birth years make a ragged left edge. The block gets wider towards the bottom because laureates are getting older: the average age at the prize goes from 55 years (1901–25) to 67 years (2001–26).
- **Colours:** women are drawn in red and organisations as dotted blue lines.
- **Labels:** a few laureates are named, including Marie Curie, Malala Yousafzai and John B. Goodenough. The Friends Service Council (founded 1647) runs off the left edge of the page and is labelled as doing so.
- **Other details:** the war years when no prizes were given show as a gap. The page also has faint guide lines for constant ages and a small bar chart of the average age by period.

`NOTE.md` in the same folder has the title and a short paragraph on what the form does and why I chose it.
