Each laureate is one horizontal line that starts at the year they were born (or, for organisations, founded) and ends at the year of their prize. Rows go down the page by prize year, so all the line ends fall on one diagonal, while the birth ends trail further and further behind it. Men are grey, women red and organisations blue. A brown line marks the median birth year, which works out to a median age at award of 56 in the early years and 69 now.
