I made v4. In v3 the pieces were piled by share, so a missing prize only lowered a column's ragged top. Now each prize is fixed to its category's row, and the six rows are Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Peace and Economics. v3's money-wide years stay, and so do the pieces whose area is the money each laureate took home.

- **(a) Functions in one structure:** the prize count and the category become one thing, the row. Each cell is one prize, so its position says which prize it is and a hole says it was not given.
- **(b) Obstacle turned into a means:** the absences now draw themselves in their own rows: the war years, the withheld Peace prizes and the empty Economics row before 1969. The split into thirds and the women also land where they belong. The science rows are striped, Literature is mostly solid with many red pieces, and Peace is red and blue.
- **(c) Struck:** the "N prizes" scale, the sort by share and its legend line, and the "1969 adds a sixth prize" and "2001, the richest" notes.

The right margin counts each row's prizes not given: Physics 6, Chemistry 8, Medicine 9, Literature 7, Peace 19, Economics 0. These include years when no prize was given at all; such years take no width, so their holes are counted but not drawn, and the footer says so. The heading, the three lines and a short paragraph are under `## v4` in `NOTE.md`, and `index.html` is updated.
