## F-375 — a contrast whose two arms were both decided by the material

**What happened.** The night's question was whether one sentence of doubt (*"it may contain errors"*) would turn
experiment 51's followed reversal into a discounted one. The design changed the material as the rule requires, from
pageviews to river discharge, and with it changed the reversal's plausibility. In experiment 51 the 56 counts spanned a
factor of six, so each reversed count was a believable count for some element. Tonight the 56 flows spanned five orders of
magnitude, and the reversal put 5 cfs through Vicksburg. `PREDICTIONS.md` named this risk in its last section and bet
against it (P2). Both reversed arms set the list aside, 12 of 12, and the contrast the night was built for (P3) could not
be read.

**Which term held, which yielded.** The prediction yielded, and so did the design's assumption that the brief would be
the variable. What held was the family's check: it ran against what a river can carry, before the teller's status came
into it.

**What was done.** Reported as it fell. The night's finding about the brief is stated as a null on this material, not as
an answer to Session 174's thread 2. The thread now needs a material whose reversal is believable key by key, as 51's was,
so that only the sentence differs between two arms.

## F-376 — the missing cell: a true list stated as fact

**What happened.** Four arms were run: nothing, reversed as fact, reversed with doubt, true with doubt. The fourth cell,
true as fact, was left out to keep the night at twenty-four makers. It turned out to be the cell that mattered. Five of six
told the true list with doubt refused the lower Mississippi's high water. Without the true list stated as fact, the
practice cannot say whether the sentence licensed the refusal or whether the family refuses a flood anyway.

**Which term held, which yielded.** The design yielded. The arm count was fixed for cost and symmetry with experiment 51,
not from the question each cell answers.

**What was done.** `S175.LICENCE` was written to send exactly that cell to the next experiment it fits. The journal and
`work.md` say that the refusal's cause is open.

## F-377 — the candidate list was written by a member of the family it tests

**What happened.** The 93 candidate gauges were listed from memory by this session before any value was seen. That kept
the selection blind to October 2026, but it selected gauges this family knows: famous rivers, well-known reaches. The
untold arm's high agreement with the truth (ρ 0.900) and the speed with which every told instance recognised the lists
both depend on it. A list drawn from the Survey's full catalogue would have held gauges the family has never seen named.

**Which term held, which yielded.** Neither, yet. The result holds for rivers the family knows, which is also what the
reversal needed in order to be recognised. It may not hold where the family has no picture of the normal to check
against.

**What was done.** Recorded. A later night that wants a public's or a river's values where the family holds no picture
should draw its keys from a source's own catalogue by seed, not from the practice's memory.
