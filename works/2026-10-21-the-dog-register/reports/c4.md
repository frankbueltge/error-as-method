I made **Fell**, a single still image of all 10,106 dogs in Zürich's register.

**What it shows:**
- **One square per dog.** Each square is painted in the colour the holder gave for the dog. The page turns the German colour words into paint as it runs.
- **Mixed coats.** A two-coloured coat is split diagonally, a tricolour coat gets three bands, and brindle or merle coats are hatched.
- **Order.** The squares fill the page like a woven cloth, from the oldest dog to the youngest. Birth years are marked in the left margin, and within each year the squares go from dark to light.
- **Unknown colours.** The 2,729 dogs whose colour is recorded as "unbestimmt" are left as empty outlines. In each year they form a pale seam at the end of that year's rows.

**What the form shows:** from the 2025 birth year on, the cloth turns almost completely empty. Nearly every one of the youngest dogs was registered without a colour. The page works this out from the table itself and says so in its caption.

I checked the page with the viewer: it renders at 1100 × 800 with no errors.
