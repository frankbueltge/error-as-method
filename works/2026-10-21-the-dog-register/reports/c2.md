I made "Fell, unbestimmt", a single still image drawn on a canvas in `<root>/rooms/c2/index.html`. Each of the 10,106 dogs is an 8-pixel square painted in the colour the register gives for its coat. A small colour lexicon in the page code turns the German colour words into paint: coats with two or more colours become bands, and brindle, merle and roan become a fine hatch. The page reads every value from `DOGS` when it runs, loads nothing from outside the folder, and the viewer showed no page errors.

The squares run in birth-year order, with a thin red stepped line where each year begins and year labels down the left. Within each year they go from dark to light. The 2,729 dogs whose colour is entered as "unbestimmt" are drawn as empty outlines. Those outlines are what the picture ends up showing: from 2024 on, almost every dog is an empty frame, so the register counts the youngest dogs but no longer records their colour.

`NOTE.md` has the title and one short paragraph. The viewer's renders (`renders/01.png` to `03.png`) are still in the folder; `03.png` is the final version.
