I made "Fell, unbestimmt" (<root>/rooms/c3/index.html, with the note in <root>/rooms/c3/NOTE.md). It is a still image drawn on a canvas from `DOGS` when the page runs. The viewer rendered it with no page errors; the last picture is <root>/rooms/c3/renders/02.png.

**The form:** each of the 10,106 dogs is one small square, painted in the coat colour the register gives it. For this, the page has its own list that turns the German colour words into colours. A coat recorded as several colours ("schwarz/weiss", "tricolor") is split into bands. The squares are stacked in one column per birth year, 2002 to 2026, so the columns together make a histogram built from dogs. Inside each column the darkest coats sit at the bottom and the lightest above them. The 2,729 dogs whose coat is recorded only as "unbestimmt" go on top, each as an empty square with a single dot.

**What it shows:** the 2020–21 bulge is clear in the column heights. The striking part is at the right edge: almost every dog born in 2024 and every dog born in 2025 or 2026 has no coat colour recorded yet. Those columns are almost all dots, so the register is behind on its youngest dogs.
