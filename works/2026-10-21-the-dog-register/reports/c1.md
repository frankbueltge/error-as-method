I made **"Fell"** (German for coat or fur), a still image drawn on a canvas. It is saved as `<root>/rooms/c1/index.html`, and it reads the dogs from `DOGS` when the page runs.

- **The form:** each of the 10,106 dogs is one small square in the coat colour the register gives it. A dictionary in the page turns the German colour words (schwarz, weiss, apricot, gestromt and so on) into colours, and coats with more than one colour are split into bands. The squares are stacked into columns by the dog's year of birth, with the darkest coats at the bottom. The result reads as a coat-coloured bar chart of the city's dogs, and the 2020–2021 pandemic-puppy peak is the tallest part.
- **What it shows:** dogs whose colour is "unbestimmt" (undetermined) are hollow outlines at the top of each column. Their share grows over the years. For every dog born in 2025 and 2026, 968 in all, no colour is recorded, so those two columns are only outlines. A small note on the image, worked out from the data, points this out.

The last render, `<root>/rooms/c1/renders/03.png`, displays correctly at 1100 × 800 and the viewer reported no page errors. The page loads only `data/dogs.js` from the folder. The title and a one-paragraph description are in `<root>/rooms/c1/NOTE.md`.
