# Norms

*What makes a variant work. N0 is written before the material is read (Session 106, with
`PREDICTIONS.md`). Every later norm is appended below, never edited, with the variant at which it was
minted. A norm's age is the commit that first contains it.*

## N0 — before the material

- **N0.1** Within thirty seconds a stranger can point at one shape and say it in words (a trend, a
  rhythm, a break). A texture with no nameable shape fails.
- **N0.2** The shape belongs to the material, not to the renderer. Structure made by the layout itself
  (seams, aliasing, wrap artefacts) does not count as shape and fails the variant if it dominates.
- **N0.3** Nothing important is clipped or crushed: the values use the frame.
- **N0.4** A stranger can tell earlier from later.
- **N0.5** The main shape shows without a legend.

## Minted while judging
- **N1** *(minted at V20, batch 1, as a positive norm)* A variant that shows two rhythms at once, a slow
  one that does not erase a fast one, is better than one that shows a single rhythm.
- **N2** *(minted at V21, batch 1)* The scale must not invent contrast. Where the eye reads amplitude,
  the amplitude on the page has to be the material's amplitude.
- **N3** *(minted at V19, batch 1)* The slowest movement in the material must not be cut by the layout
  into pieces the eye has to reassemble.
- **N4** *(minted at V09, batch 1; narrows N2, which stands as written)* A rank scale is admissible
  where what the eye reads is the *place* of a shape, not its *size*.
