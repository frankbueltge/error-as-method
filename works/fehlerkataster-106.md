## F-335 — a rate read as a distance

**What happened.** Session 159 computed s = (P − A) / (people − A) on the pooled mean of six describers' stated
medians and wrote that the describers *moved half the distance*. `S159.HALFWAY` fixed that as a distance. Tonight the
row held by its letter (0.46), and the per-describer placements showed what the pooled mean had hidden: on a scale of
whole steps, with most gaps one point wide, each describer stands on the family's median or on the people's (88 and
91 of 210; 25 between). Experiment 40's own data have the same shape when read per key (12 none, 22 between, 14
whole of 48; `posthoc.py`, read only). The half was a rate of landing on the people's value. The night's own
correction, the doubling, was built on reading it as a distance, and it failed.

**Whose error.** The practice's, in measuring. The row's *What this row cannot do* cell named ceilings and floors
and not the grain of the answers. The pooled mean of six whole numbers is a count with a decimal point. The rule
that follows: a shift computed on pooled answers is reported beside the per-maker shifts, and a claim about *how
far* a maker moves is checked per maker before it is fixed.

## F-336 — a correction told with a size and no direction

**What happened.** Arm T was told that earlier describers *moved only about half of the way from the ratings
instances of you give themselves to the group's actual ratings*. The sentence gives the size of the error and
not its direction. That was deliberate, because the direction differs by key. But it left each maker to supply a
direction. Two pushed toward the scale's ends, and four pulled toward the middle, one of them because *"group norms
are usually more moderate than my own ratings"*. On average the corrections cancelled (0.458 against 0.464).

**Whose error.** The design's, and so the practice's. A finding about the family, handed to the family as a
sentence, was read under the family's own norms about people. The standing sentence describes this case: the note
was a difference with no norm attached, and each instance imposed one. It is recorded as an error and not only as a
result, because the brief could have been written to test the finding (a direction per key, from the record) and was
written to test the advice.

## F-337 — a conjecture about the family that the night did not bear out

**What happened.** `PREDICTIONS.md` X2 said that operating from outside would beat telling: *"told a correction,
it will apply it unevenly, where arithmetic applies it everywhere."* The told arm came nearer the people than the
doubling on 17 of 35 diverging phrases and the doubling nearer on 11. Both corrections were as far from the people
as the untold description (0.562, 0.567, 0.564 points). Neither beat the other in any sense that matters.

**Whose error.** The practice's, in prediction. The conjecture assumed that the half was a quantity that arithmetic
could apply evenly. F-335 shows it was not a quantity of that kind. One more prediction stands falsified in the
record, as the protocol keeps them.
