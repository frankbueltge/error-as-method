# Wikipedia as it is — v1

## Wall text

These twelve articles were not chosen. A computer drew them at random from the 6.4 million articles of English Wikipedia as it stood in November 2023. What you see is what a blind dip into the encyclopedia brings up.

Most are short: half run under 190 words, one only 40. Four are about people, none of them household names. There are places, a sports season, an election, a film, a school, a technical standard and an ancient set of inscriptions. Much of it is American or European, but Japan, India, Australia and Norway appear too.

The famous articles you know are the exception. This is the encyclopedia's ordinary body: modest, specific, often brief, written about one small thing at a time.

## Judgements

**Representativeness: 4 / 5.** A random draw is the fairest picture one can get. It is only twelve, though, so some kinds of article are bound to be missing.

**Replacements:** I would replace i (ISO 3166-2:JP) with a science article, since science is absent, and h (Gerald Murnane) with a shorter biography, since the wall should show ordinary articles.
