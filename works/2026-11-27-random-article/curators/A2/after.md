# After: estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

These are estimates from memory, without looking anything up.

1. About a person: about 20%
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: about 8%
3. About a species or other taxon: about 6%
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: about 5%
5. Under 100 words long: about 15%

Did these estimates play any part in the tags? Only loosely: I tagged from a general sense that biographies, places and short pages are common, not from any figures, and I never compared the draw with estimates like these. If I had, I might have noticed that the draw has no taxon at all and that two of its twelve pages are under 100 words, which fits the roughly 15% estimate above.
