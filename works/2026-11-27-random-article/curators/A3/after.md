# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole (no lookup)

1. About a person: about 22%
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: about 11%
3. About a species or other taxon: about 7%
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: about 8% (roughly 5% disambiguation, 3% lists)
5. Under 100 words long: about 15%

Did these play a part in the tags? Yes, though only as rough, unspoken versions: I tagged the signpost pages and the list `unusual` because I believed they were a small minority, and tagged people and places `typical` because I believed they were large groups. I never set these numbers against the draw; had I done so, I would have seen that four of the twelve are under 100 words against my 15%, and three are signposts against my 5%.
