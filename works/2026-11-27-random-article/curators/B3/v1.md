# Wikipedia as it is — v1

## Wall text

These twelve pages were picked by a computer, not by us. Together they show what English Wikipedia mostly is: short, specific and obscure. Half run under 250 words. Three are not articles about anything, only signposts listing other pages that share a name. There is a village in Burma, a monorail station in Japan, an English district abolished in 1974, a record producer, a screenwriter, a joke book. None of these subjects is famous, and that is the point: the encyclopedia is built from millions of small entries like these, not from the pages you usually visit. They hang from shortest to longest.

## Captions

**j. Katano Station** (40 words) — `typical`

**l. Queensbury and Shelf Urban District** (124 words) — `typical`

## Judgements

**How well these twelve represent English Wikipedia:** 3 of 5.

Their lengths match the 160 closely (median about 245 words here, about 210 there), and the mix of places, people and stubs is right. But signpost pages are three of twelve here against about one in eleven in the 160, and there is no sport at all (about one page in eight in the 160) and no plant or animal species.

**What I would replace:**
- **i. Monteils** → a sports page, such as a minor athlete or a season of a club. Sport is the largest missing category, and the wall already has two other signpost pages.
