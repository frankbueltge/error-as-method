# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

1. About a person: **about 22%**
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: **about 13%**
3. About a species or other taxon: **about 6%**
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: **about 12%** (roughly 8% disambiguation, 4% lists)
5. Under 100 words long: **about 25%**

Did these estimates play any part in the tags? Yes, though mostly indirectly. I tagged mainly from shares I counted in the 160 (biographies about a fifth, signposts about one in eleven, broad-concept pages and long narrow lists rare), and these overall estimates sit close to those counts. My background sense of these proportions decided borderline calls: it was why I tagged the three signpost pages and the short place stubs `typical` rather than treating thinness as unusual.
