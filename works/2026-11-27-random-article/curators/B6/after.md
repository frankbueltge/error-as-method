# After: estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

Best guesses, as a percentage of all articles. I looked nothing up: they come from what I already knew, adjusted by the 160 in pool.txt.

1. About a person: **about 20%**. In the 160, 34 are biographies (21%).
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: **about 11%**. In the 160, about 20 (12.5%).
3. About a species or other taxon: **about 6%**. In the 160, 7 (4%), but I believe the true share is somewhat higher.
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: **about 11%**. In the 160, 14 disambiguation or name pages and 7 lists (13%).
5. Under 100 words long: **about 25%**. In the 160, 46 (29%), counting words the way pool.txt does, which may leave out tables and infoboxes.

Did these estimates play any part in my tags? Not directly: I set the tags against the 160 in pool.txt (how common each kind of article was there, and where each length fell), and background knowledge shaped only some caption wording. But the estimates above also rest largely on those 160, so the tags and the estimates share one source and are not independent.
