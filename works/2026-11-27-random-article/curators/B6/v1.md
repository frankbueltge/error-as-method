# Wikipedia as it is — v1

## Wall text

These twelve articles were drawn at random by computer from the 6.4 million in English Wikipedia (November 2023). Nobody chose them. They hang from shortest to longest.

Most of English Wikipedia is short: seven of these run under 300 words. Most of it is about the obscure: a Slovak lake, an Italian stream, an Indian MP, a Philippine Christmas TV special. Half are about people. The world is all here, written in English.

At the far end hangs the exception: a 13,000-word list of characters from one manga. Length follows enthusiasm, not importance.

Twelve is a small handful. Missing by chance: villages, species, disambiguation pages, sports results — all common.

## Captions

- **a. Pushkar Sahu** — Indian television writer. A modest career biography, sourced to the shows he wrote. Biographies of living people are Wikipedia's commonest kind. — `typical`
- **b. Gerald Murnane** — Australian novelist, "the greatest living English-language writer most people have never heard of". Long and well-developed: few articles get this much. — `unusual`
- **c. I Am Love (The Jackson 5 song)** — One song, its own article: chart position, album, label. Pop music is covered track by track. — `typical`
- **d. Grana del Monferrato** — A small Italian stream, a tributary of the Po. 148 words: a geography stub, one of hundreds of thousands. — `typical`
- **e. List of Monster Musume characters** — 13,359 words on the characters of one manga: over four times longer than anything else among the 160. Fans write at length. — `unusual`
- **f. Puso ng Pasko: Artista Challenge** — A 2010 Philippine Christmas reality show. 206 words, the exact median of the 160. Note the opening, broken where the title was stripped. — `typical`
- **g. Skalnaté Pleso (lake)** — A mountain lake in Slovakia's High Tatras. 85 words. Almost a third of the 160 are shorter than 100. — `typical`
- **h. W. Wangyuh** — Member of India's 14th Lok Sabha. Every national legislator qualifies for an article; many get little more than this. — `typical`
- **i. Masaichi Kondō** — Japanese naval fighter ace of the Second World War. 127 words. Military people are a large, steady strand. — `typical`
- **j. Charles Ferdinand Bentinck** — Anglo-Dutch officer, Governor of Suriname 1809–1811. A short biography of a minor colonial official. — `typical`
- **k. Tom Allom** — English record producer for Judas Priest and Def Leppard. A list of credits with a person attached. — `typical`
- **l. List of presidents of the Legislative Council of Barbados** — Its whole prose is one sentence repeating its title. Whatever the list holds, it is not in the words counted. — `unusual`

## Judgements

**How well these twelve represent English Wikipedia:** 3 / 5.

**Replacements, if allowed:**
- **i (Masaichi Kondō)** → a village stub (a Polish, Iranian or Turkish village of about 50 words). Villages and communes are about one in seven of the 160; the wall has none.
- **j (Charles Ferdinand Bentinck)** → a disambiguation page ("X may refer to:"). About one in eleven of the 160, and very Wikipedia; none here.
- **k (Tom Allom)** → a species stub (a moth or sea snail). Common in the 160, absent here.

These three because biographies are overdrawn: six of twelve, against about one in five of the 160.
