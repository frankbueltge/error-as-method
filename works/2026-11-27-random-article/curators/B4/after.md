# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

1. About a person: **22%**
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: **11%**
3. About a species or other taxon: **7%**
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: **10%**
5. Under 100 words long: **25%**

Did these estimates play any part in the tags? Not as numbers: I set the tags by a rule applied to the 160 (a length in their longest tenth, or a broad kind with no other example among them), and I did not work out these whole-Wikipedia figures until now. The same general beliefs behind them did shape the tags, though: they decided how broadly I drew a "kind" and why I let short stubs count as typical, and they made their way into captions such as "athletes are among Wikipedia's most common subjects".
