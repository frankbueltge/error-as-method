# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

1. About a person: **25%**
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: **13%**
3. About a species or other taxon: **6%**
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: **12%** (about 8% disambiguation, 4% lists)
5. Under 100 words long: **28%**

These are best guesses. They mix what I already knew with a rough count of the 160-article pool, which gave about 28% people, 15% settlements, 4% taxa, 13% disambiguation pages and lists, and 29% under 100 words.

Did these estimates shape the tags? Only loosely. I never wrote down percentages while tagging. I tagged on a general sense of which kinds of article are common, plus where each article's length sat in the pool (so I called the 1,100+ word pages unusual), and these numbers are a written-out version of that sense, made afterwards.
