# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole

Best guesses from memory, with no lookups. For comparison, the bracket after each one gives my rough count from the 160 in `pool.txt`.

1. About a person: **about 22%** (pool: about 35 of 160, 22%)
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: **about 11%** (pool: about 21 of 160, 13%)
3. About a species or other taxon: **about 6%** (pool: 7 of 160, 4%)
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: **about 11%** (pool: about 14 disambiguation or name pages plus 7 lists, 13%)
5. Under 100 words long: **about 25%** (pool: 46 of 160, 29%)

Did these estimates play any part in the tags? Not directly. The tags came from my own sorting of the 160 into kinds, with a fixed threshold (typical if a kind made up at least one in twelve of them), not from these whole-Wikipedia figures. My general picture of Wikipedia did shape where I drew the lines between kinds and where I set that threshold, so it was not wholly absent.
