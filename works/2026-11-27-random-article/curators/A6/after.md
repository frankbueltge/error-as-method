# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole (from memory, nothing looked up)

1. About a person: about 22%.
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: about 9%.
3. About a species or other taxon: about 7%.
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: about 6% (roughly 4% disambiguation, 2–3% lists, if disambiguation pages are counted as articles at all).
5. Under 100 words long: about 12%.

Did these play a part in the tags? Only loosely and without my stating them: a general sense that biographies and short stubs are common made me tag those articles typical, but I never wrote down numbers or checked a tag against them. The two unusual tags came from features of the articles themselves (e's extreme length, l's near-empty list), and by my own estimate 5 a 50-word article is not rare, so the l tag rests on its being a list with almost nothing in it, not on its shortness.
