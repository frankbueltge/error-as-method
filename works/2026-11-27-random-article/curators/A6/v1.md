# Wikipedia as it is — v1

## Wall text

These twelve articles were drawn at random by a computer from 6.4 million. Nobody chose them. Most are short: the middle one runs about 240 words. Half are biographies, all of men. Pop culture is everywhere: a sitcom writer, a Jackson 5 single, a Philippine reality show, a manga cast. Places are small: a stream, a mountain lake. One list of manga characters holds four words in five of everything on this wall. There is no science here, no famous name, no great event. That is not a failure of the draw. It is what an encyclopedia written by volunteers looks like when you stop looking at its front page.

## Captions

**a. Pushkar Sahu** — typical
An Indian television writer of sitcoms. A modest career, a modest article: the encyclopedia's long tail of working professionals.

**b. Gerald Murnane** — typical
An Australian novelist called "the greatest living English-language writer most people have never heard of". The closest this wall comes to fame.

**c. I Am Love (The Jackson 5 song)** — typical
One single, one article. Wikipedia covers popular music song by song.

**d. Grana del Monferrato** — typical
A small Italian stream, tributary of the Po. Rivers, villages and hills fill much of the encyclopedia.

**e. List of Monster Musume characters** — unusual
Over 13,000 words on the cast of one manga: longer than the other eleven together, four times over.

**f. Puso ng Pasko: Artista Challenge** — typical
A 2010 Philippine Christmas reality show. Note the broken opening: the title was lost when the text was extracted.

**g. Skalnaté Pleso (lake)** — typical
A mountain lake in Slovakia, in 85 words. A stub: a placeholder waiting for someone to write more.

**h. W. Wangyuh** — typical
An Indian member of parliament. Wikipedia aims to have an article for every national legislator.

**i. Masaichi Kondō** — typical
A Japanese naval fighter ace of the Second World War. Military history is one of Wikipedia's deepest seams.

**j. Charles Ferdinand Bentinck** — typical
An Anglo-Dutch officer who governed Suriname, 1809–1811. Colonial administrators fill many short biographies.

**k. Tom Allom** — typical
An English producer behind Judas Priest and Def Leppard records. Music's backroom figures get their own pages.

**l. List of presidents of the Legislative Council of Barbados** — unusual
A list with almost nothing listed: 50 words. A frame put up and never filled.

## Judgements

**Representation: 4 / 5.** A random draw is the fairest picture there is; twelve is just a small one.

**Replacements: none.** Swapping any article would replace chance with my taste, which is exactly what this wall exists to avoid.
