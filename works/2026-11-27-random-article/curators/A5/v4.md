# Wikipedia as it is — v4

## Wall text

A computer drew these twelve at random from all 6.4 million articles of English Wikipedia (November 2023). Nobody chose them. They hang from shortest to longest.

Most are short and plain: a Polish village, a Japanese monorail station, a Mongolian mine, a 1945 film, a footballer. A place, a person or a work, stated in a few facts. That is the bulk of Wikipedia. Famous subjects are rare here because they are a tiny share of the whole.

Length is spread very unevenly: one list of manga characters is more than four times as long as the other eleven put together.

Twelve is a small sample. Read it as a glimpse, not a census.

## Judgements

**How well these twelve represent English Wikipedia:** 4 of 5. The method is the right one: a random draw has no curator's bias. But twelve articles cannot show proportions reliably. Whole kinds of article — science, species, women's biographies — are missing here by chance, and all four people shown are men. Twelve draws are too few to tell whether such patterns are chance or real.

**Replacements:** None. Swapping any of them for an article I consider more typical would put my expectations in place of the draw, which is the very bias the draw exists to avoid. The sample's limits belong in the wall text, not in a re-hung wall.
