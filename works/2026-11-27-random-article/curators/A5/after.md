# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole (no lookup)

1. About a person: about 28%
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: about 9%
3. About a species or other taxon: about 7%
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: about 7% (roughly 4% disambiguation, 3% lists)
5. Under 100 words long: about 15%

Did these estimates play any part in the tags? Only implicitly: I did not work out any numbers while tagging, but I tagged from a rough sense that short articles on people, places and works make up most of Wikipedia. The one `unusual` tag (the manga character list) rested on its extreme length, not on its being a list.
