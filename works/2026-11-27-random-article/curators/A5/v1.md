# Wikipedia as it is — v1

## Wall text

These twelve articles were drawn at random by a computer from all 6.4 million in English Wikipedia (November 2023). No one picked them. Most are short: a Polish village, a Japanese monorail station, a Mongolian mine, a 1945 film, a footballer, an Indian politician. This is what most of Wikipedia is: brief, factual entries on places, people and things, many written from lists and databases. Famous subjects are rare in a random draw because they are a tiny share of the whole. One article here runs to over 13,000 words — a list of manga characters — a reminder that length is spread very unevenly.
