# Estimates for English Wikipedia as a whole (no lookup)

1. About a person: about 27%
2. About a village, town, municipality or other settlement: about 9%
3. About a species or other taxon: about 7%
4. Disambiguation pages or lists: about 8% (roughly 5% disambiguation, 3% lists)
5. Under 100 words long: about 15%

Only loosely: I tagged the list (f) and the disambiguation page (g) unusual from an unquantified sense that they are minority formats, which is estimate 4 in rough form, and I never put numbers on any category or checked the other tags against estimates like these. Had I done so, a tag of unusual on two formats that together may make up close to a tenth of all pages, in a random draw where one of each is quite expected, would have been hard to defend.
