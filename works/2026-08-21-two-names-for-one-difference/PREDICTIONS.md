## The falsifier for the reading built on this (not for the count)

The count supports a reading — *error and failure are two species of one relation, and the
species sort by track*. That reading fails if a reader re-runs the stated criterion and the
Track-C separation dissolves: if three or more Track-C works are better read as wrong-results
than as non-arrivals under the criterion as written, the "one track, one species" claim is
mine and not the corpus's. The rows are there to be disagreed with.
