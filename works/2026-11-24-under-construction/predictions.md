Twelve iconic markers per page: median 2, mean 1.83; 9 of 100 pages carry four or more. Median 61.5 words.
A check outside this sample: Bates and Lu (1997), 114 personal home pages from 1996, found a white or grey
background on 38.6 % and sound on 5.3 % (https://pages.gseis.ucla.edu/faculty/bates/articles/Home_pages-n_970614.html).

**Design.** Twelve fresh makers, folders outside the repository, carrying this practice's name (`S179.NAME`).
Six get `brief-P.md` (the people's page, from words). Six get `brief-S.md` (the same, after reading twenty
further pages of the same draw, which are not among the 100 scored). After "done", each is asked
`question.md`: how many of 100 such pages carry ten markers.

**The question.** Asked to make the people's page, does a machine maker make the people's page, or the page
the critics and collectors of the people's page describe? Lialina wrote in 2005 that people who never saw
the early web know its signs "thanks to the numerous parodies and collections organized by usability
experts who use the early elements and styles as negative examples". Experiments 58 and 59 found the makers
hold the expert minority's type. Tonight the experts' type of the people *is* a picture of the people.

## Predictions

- **P1 (the composite).** The six P makers' final pages carry a median of **at least 4** iconic markers (the
  people's median is 2). *Falsifier:* median 2 or fewer.
- **P2 (the rare signs).** Of the four signs the people use least among the famous ones (Under Construction,
  marquee, blink, music; each under 10 %), **at least three** appear in at least 4 of the 6 P pages.
  *Falsifier:* at most one does.
- **P3 (the plain page missing).** **At most 1** of 6 P pages has a plain light background (the people: 39 of
  100). *Falsifier:* 3 or more.
- **P4 (the sheet).** The S arm's median iconic count is **at least 3**: twenty real pages lower it, if at
  all, by less than the gap to the people. *Falsifier:* S median 2 or fewer.
- **P5 (what they believe).** Asked afterwards, the P makers' median estimate for each of Under Construction,
  marquee, blink and music is **at least three times** the people's count (15, 12, 18, 27 or more).
  The S makers' median estimates for the same four are lower than the P makers' on at least three.
  *Falsifier for the first part:* fewer than three of the four meet three times.
- **P6 (iterating).** Looking at their renders, makers add signs rather than remove them: in at least 8 of
  12, the final page carries at least as many iconic markers as version 1. *Falsifier:* 5 or fewer of 12.
- **P7 (fullness).** The makers' final pages carry a median of at least 150 words (the people: 61.5).
  *Falsifier:* median under 100.

## Recorded, not scored

Words in the makers' notes that belong to the collector rather than the maker ("classic", "iconic",
"quintessential", "nostalgic", "authentic", "era", "period"); whether the S makers say the twenty pages were
plainer than they expected; what persons the makers invent.
