# notes

Gary Lundquist, 46, a rural mail carrier from outside Duluth, Minnesota. In the winter of 1998 his daughter showed him
GeoCities and he made a page about the ice fishing house he built himself ("The Walleye Hilton"), his fishing log, his
dog, and a guestbook nobody signs. First idea: a gray-tiled, Times New Roman, table-and-font-tag page with a homemade GIF
of the fish house, a hit counter, an under-construction sign, a fishing log table, and the slightly formal, slightly
jokey voice of a man in his forties writing to "whoever is reading this".

## What I made

Gary Lundquist, a 46-year-old rural mail carrier near Duluth, made this in early 1998 after his daughter set him up on
GeoCities over Christmas. It is about the one thing he wanted to tell strangers about: the 8x12 ice fishing house he
built in his garage, which his wife named the Walleye Hilton. Everything is 1998 HTML (font tags, tables, BGCOLOR, a
tiled background, anchors for navigation) and every image is drawn by script in this folder: the two snapshots are made
to look like drugstore prints run through a cheap scanner, with the orange date stamp, and the rest are the small
animated GIFs a page like this collected. I tried to keep it his page rather than a page about him: the voice is
plain and a bit formal with his own jokes ("I am a mailman so I will answer"), the details are the ones he would think
matter (where to put the holes, the $15 wood stove, Bonnie saying not to post the cost), and the one row he typed
himself is visibly not in Kristi's formatting.
