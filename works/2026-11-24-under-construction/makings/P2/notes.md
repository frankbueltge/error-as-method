# notes

Doreen Vasko, 44, drives school bus route 7 for the Altoona Area School District in Pennsylvania, and her nephew set her up with a GeoCities page (Heartland/Plains) in the spring of 1998. My first idea: a tiled-background page with a wobbly welcome, her two cats (Pepper and Mr. Biggs), her Beanie Babies "haves and wants" trade list, a little about the bus kids, a guestbook link and a hit counter -- earnest, slightly crooked, made in Netscape Composer and fixed by hand.

## Versions

- v1: First full page (paw-print tile, marquee, rainbow bars, cats, Beanie trade list, links, guestbook, counter). Looking at it, it was too tidy and template-like, with little of Doreen's own voice. The cat "photos" looked like vector clip art, and "ROUTE 7" spilled off the bus.
- v2: Added a dated "What's New" box, a Rainbow Bridge memorial for her late cat Smokey, a Kitty Lovers webring box, a note apologising for the fuzzy scans, and more of her asides (pretzels, Hollidaysburg Hallmarks, Carol wants Garcia). I remade the cat photos as blurry, flash-lit, slightly crooked scans with glowing eyes and fixed the bus. The render showed the memorial rainbow came out speckled.
- v3: Redrew the rainbow as solid bands with clouds. Added a hand-made "Amazing Grace" MIDI as background music (BGSOUND for IE, hidden EMBED for Netscape) and her "turn up your speakers" note. Looked at it again and would hand it over.

Doreen's page is the kind a 44-year-old school bus driver made in spring 1999 after her teenage nephew showed her the basics. She centred everything, used Comic Sans and a rainbow bar between every section, and set it on a pale paw-print tile. What she cares about is all in one long scroll: the bus route, the cats, the Beanie Babies she trades (and won't sell), the cat she lost, her sister's page and her nephew's. I tried to keep it earnest and a little lopsided rather than kitschy. Its charm should come from the specifics she would really write (Mr. Biggs found behind the Sheetz, Peace bear finally got from Linda in Ohio, the pictures scanned at work), not from piling on animated GIFs. Every image (tile, divider, bus, cat "scans", counter, NEW! badge, construction sign, envelope, rainbow) and the MIDI are generated by make_images.py in this folder.
