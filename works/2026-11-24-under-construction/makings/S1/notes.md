# notes

## The person and the first idea

Dwayne Hoberg, 46, runs the parts counter at a farm-implement dealer outside Salina, Kansas, and for eleven years has kept a colony of purple martins on two martin houses and a gourd rack in his back lot. His nephew showed him GeoCities in the winter of 1998, and the page he made is really a page about his martins: when the first scouts came back each spring, how many pairs nested, the trouble with sparrows and the hawk, and an open invitation for other "landlords" to write him. My first idea is a light-blue background, a big homemade title graphic, a yearly arrival table, a long chatty update at the top, and the usual counter, guestbook and "last updated" at the bottom.

## What I noticed in the twenty pages

Most of them are not "about me" at all; they are about the thing the person loved (a TV show, a band, a book, shark teeth, Transformers), and the person shows up sideways, in apologies for not updating, typos, asides in brackets, and a line of "a little about myself" near the end. The furniture repeats: a hit counter with a start date, guestbook sign/view links, "last updated", an email address at hotmail or aol, webrings, a GeoCities banner, and sometimes a midi or a marquee. Layout is mostly centered text, tables used for position, colored fonts on a background image, and a lot is broken or half-finished (pages closed down, frames, images missing, stray tags).

## Versions

- v1: first full page (title graphic, martin house drawing, what's new, colony table, tips, about me, counter, guestbook, email). Looking at it, it was too tidy and designed: Arial everywhere, evenly matched sections, nothing old left on the page, so it read as a modern idea of 1998.
- v2: took out the font faces so it falls back to the default Times that a Netscape Composer user would get, left-aligned the section headings, let the What's New keep old 1998 entries, added the GeoCities hosting line, a webring box, a note about where the early numbers came from, and a list of other landlords who wrote him, because people's pages grew by accretion and by the mail they got.
- v3: the blinking NEW! graphic was cut off on its right edge in the screenshot, so I widened it. Otherwise it looked like what he would have made, so I stopped.

## What I made

A page made by a 46-year-old parts-counter man in Kansas whose real subject is his purple martin colony, which is what most of the twenty pages were like: the person shows up through a hobby, not through a biography. It has the things he would have picked up from other pages and from his nephew (a shadowed title graphic, little flapping birds, a gradient divider bar, a blinking NEW!, an under-construction sign, a counter with a start date, guestbook links, a webring box, "best viewed with"), sitting on a cloud-tiled background, and his own plain, misspelled, specific writing: arrival dates, pair counts, the hawk, the heat wave, Carol hearing him yell. All the images are GIFs drawn by `make_images.py` in this folder; the subpage links (links, building, sparrows, guestbook, ring) point to pages that would sit beside it and are not included.
