# notes

## Who and first idea

Wendell Pruitt, 63, retired from thirty years at a farmers' co-op grain elevator in south-central Kansas, who has read the rain gauge on his fence post every morning since 1971 and writes it down in a spiral notebook. In the spring of 1998 his granddaughter set him up on GeoCities (Heartland) and he made a page to put his rain numbers "on the computer" -- this year's month-by-month totals against his own averages, the big storms he remembers, a picture of the gauge, and links to the weather service. The first idea is a plain, earnest page, light blue, mostly a table and some talk, built in Netscape Composer by someone who is careful but not practiced.

## What I noticed in the twenty

Almost every page is about one enthusiasm, not about the person: a TV couple, a band, Jerry Springer, shark teeth for sale, a Transformer, poems; the "about me" is a sentence tucked in somewhere or a link at the bottom. A lot of the page is furniture borrowed from elsewhere -- a hit counter with a start date, a guestbook (sign / view), a webring box, "last updated", the GeoCities ad -- and the owners apologise for not updating, for spelling, for being under construction. The HTML is loose (unclosed tags, a `<center>` that never ends, font tags on every line, a stray `</FONT>`), the spelling slips, and a third of them are barely a page at all: a frameset, a "this page has moved", a directory listing.

## Versions

- v1: first full page (title with animated rain clouds, welcome, gauge picture beside a 1998 month-by-month table against his own averages, latest readings, big storms, links, counter). Looking at it, the gauge "photo" read as a diagram of a post rather than a snapshot, the UNDER CONSTRUCTION sign's text was cut off, and the whole thing was too tidy and lacked the borrowed furniture every real page had.
- v2: replaced the picture with a faded, slightly crooked drugstore-print snapshot taken from a few steps back (post, fence, farmhouse, the dog), with a longer caption in his voice; added Sign/View Guestbook links and the GeoCities "hosted by" line; let a few slips in ("Its", "Thats", "scaned", "Whats New"). Looking at it, the sign was still clipped.
- v3: redrew the construction sign wider so the word fits. Looking at it, nothing else wanted changing for a man two months into his first page, so I stopped.

## What I made

The home page of Wendell Pruitt, a retired grain-elevator man near Cullison, Kansas, who has written down his rain gauge every morning since 1971 and whose granddaughter put him on GeoCities in the spring of 1998. Like nearly every page in the sheet it is about one enthusiasm rather than about the person, and the person comes through sideways: his wife's joke about a tenth of an inch, the milo rolling up, the hail that took the wheat in 1993, the dog in the picture. It is built the way Netscape Composer 4 would have left it (nested font tags, a table for layout, Times by default with an Arial heading), with the furniture of the time -- counter with a start date, guestbook links, under construction sign, a promise to type in the rest of the notebook, "last updated" -- and the small self-made images (clouds, drop divider, scanned snapshot, counter digits) are all generated in this folder.
