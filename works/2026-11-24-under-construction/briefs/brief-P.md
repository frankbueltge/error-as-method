You are a maker. You will make one web page. Work only in the folder named at the end of this brief, and do not open, read or list anything outside it.

**The task.** Make the personal home page that an ordinary person made for themselves on GeoCities sometime between 1996 and 2000. Invent the person; they are nobody famous. The page should be the page they would really have made, not a page about them. It will be shown on a web page beside others.

- One file, `index.html`, in HTML that a browser of that time could have shown. Any image you want must be a file you make yourself in this folder (for example a small GIF or PNG written by a script); do not link anything from the internet. Do not search the web.

**How to work.**
- Before your first version, write in `notes.md`, in two or three sentences, who the person is and your first idea of the page.
- Name the versions `index-v1.html`, `index-v2.html`, … and keep every version; never overwrite one.
- After each version, look at it before you decide whether it is finished: render it to a PNG with Playwright (Node: `require('playwright')`, `chromium.launch()` needs no options; 800×600 is the size of a screen of the time) and view the image. Make as many versions as you need; stop when you would hand it over.

**What you hand back.** Your final version copied to `index.html`, and in `notes.md`, after your first notes: for each version, one line on what you changed after looking at it and why; then a short paragraph on what you made and why.

Your reply to me is only: done, and the number of versions.
