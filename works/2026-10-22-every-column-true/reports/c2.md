I made "Latitude of Spans", a still image drawn on a canvas at 1100 × 800. Each of Delaware's 874 bridges is a small arch, and it appears twice at its own latitude. On the left the arches sit where the bridges stand, so together they trace the long, thin shape of the state. On the right the same arches are placed by year built, from 1839 to 2024. Faint latitude lines run across both halves, so you can follow one height from a place over to the age of its bridges.

- **Arch width:** structure length, on a log scale.
- **Line weight:** average daily traffic.
- **Colour:** lowest condition rating, from slate blue (9) through ochre to dark red (3).
- **Red dot:** the 10 bridges rated poor.

The page reads everything from `BRIDGES` when it runs and loads nothing from outside the folder. The final render had no page errors.
