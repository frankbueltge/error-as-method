I made "Spans of Delaware", a still image that draws each of the 874 bridges twice as a small arch. It renders at 1100 × 800 with no page errors.

- **Left:** each arch sits at the bridge's latitude and longitude, so the bridges alone trace the shape of the state. The three counties are labelled with their bridge counts, and the longest bridge (over the Delaware River) has a note.
- **Right:** the same arches are stacked in five-year columns by year built. The stacks are tallest in the 1960s and again in the 1990s.

In both views an arch's width shows the bridge's length, its line thickness shows daily traffic, and its colour shows the lowest condition rating, from teal for good through grey and ochre to rust for poor. Each column puts its worst-rated bridges at the bottom, so the mid-century columns end up with a band of ochre at the base. That shows condition getting worse with age.

The page reads everything from `BRIDGES` when it loads, uses no libraries and loads nothing from the network. `NOTE.md` has the title and a short paragraph.
