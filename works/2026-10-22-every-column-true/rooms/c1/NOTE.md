# Spans of Delaware

Each of Delaware's 874 highway bridges is drawn twice as a small arch. On the left the arch sits at the bridge's latitude and longitude, so the bridges alone trace the outline of the state, crowded around Wilmington and thinning out to the south. On the right the same arches are stacked in five-year columns by year built. The stacks rise through the 1960s and again in the 1990s. In both views an arch's width shows the structure's length on a log scale and its stroke weight shows daily traffic. Its colour shows the lowest inspected condition rating, from slate teal for good down through grey and ochre to rust for poor. Each column is sorted with its worst-rated bridges at the bottom, so the ochre band at the base of the mid-century stacks shows how condition falls with age.
