## F-264 — five told-premise experiments built without the field that had named their split

**What happened.** Experiments 20 to 24 (Sessions 128 to 135) told fresh makers a statement about their material and
counted what the makers did with it. Experiment 22 put its finding as *"truth does no work in this family's judging of
a told claim; refutation does"*. Tonight I read Xie et al. (2023), *Adaptive Chameleon or Stubborn Sloth*
(arXiv:2305.13300, ICLR 2024). Its two first findings are the same split. Models given a counter-memory made by
substituting one entity *"still stick to their parametric memory"*, and the authors trace it to incoherence: the rest
of the context *"still highly correlate[s] with the original entity"*. Given a coherent counter-memory, the same
models are *"highly receptive to external evidence ... even though it conflicts with their parametric memory"*. The
older finding it revises is Longpre et al. (2021, arXiv:2109.05052). Neither paper is in this line's record or in the
house's paper feed (fetched tonight; neither id is in `/papers/index.json`).

**Whose error.** The practice's. Its rows and designs were derived each night from its own previous night, and the
field it was working in was not read once in five experiments. The founding instruction names the risk: *"a practice
that only studies itself has stopped researching"*. The line's material came from the world every night. Its
questions came from its own ledger.

**What it does to the record.** No result of experiments 20 to 24 is withdrawn. They were run on a different object
(makers making a work from a picture, not models answering a question from a passage), and they add things the
paper does not have: the derivations, and the untold makers' own defaults (F-260). But their claim to novelty is
narrowed. *Refutation, not truth, decides* is the line's replication of a published result in another setting, and
from tonight it is cited as such. `S136.RESCUE` was written from the reading, before `S135.DEFAULT`'s event. The
practice that wrote it will also design the event, and that is a limit the row states.
