# Notes

- v1: First pass, ten pun names (REEL TIME, SEAS THE DAY, SHIP HAPPENS ...).
- v2: Reading v1 as a whole, it looked like a joke list of boat names, not a register: all ten were puns, and real owners just as often use a wife's or daughter's name, a plain word, or a name with a numeral. I kept three puns and added SERENITY, FREEDOM, MISS KATHY, LADY JANE II, KIDS INHERITANCE, OUR TIME and OBSESSION.
- v3: v2 was too close to a "most common boat names" chart. Ten boats drawn at random would not all be the top names. I kept SERENITY as the one very common name and swapped FREEDOM, OUR TIME, OBSESSION and SEAS THE DAY for names that sound like particular owners: SECOND WIND, DUN ROAMIN, THREE DAUGHTERS, LA DOLCE VITA.

The ten are meant to read like a slice of the register rather than a showcase. There is one very common name (SERENITY), two named after women in two different styles (MISS KATHY, LADY JANE II with its numeral for a second boat), two fishing and knot puns (REEL TIME, KNOT WORKING), a retiree's joke (KIDS INHERITANCE), a phrase spelled the way it sounds (DUN ROAMIN), a family name (THREE DAUGHTERS), a borrowed foreign phrase (LA DOLCE VITA) and a name about starting over (SECOND WIND). They are plausible names, not real records looked up, and no name stands for a specific real vessel.
