# Notes

- v1: First pass. On rereading, it was all puns. It read like a "funny boat names" listicle, not like ten real sterns.
- v2: Replaced most of the puns with plain, sincere names that owners really choose (SERENITY, FREEDOM, SECOND WIND, MISS KATHLEEN, ANDIAMO). On rereading, it was now a list of the ten most common names: right in kind, but too tidy for a random draw of ten.
- v3: Kept a few of the very common names and added the odd ones a real sample would include: a coined family name (JAKAMA, made from the children's first syllables), a numeral for a second boat with the same name (SECOND WIND II), a money joke from a retiree (PAID IN FULL), and a plain OUR TIME. Dropped FREEDOM, ANDIAMO, LIQUID ASSET and SEAS THE DAY so no one register dominates. I would hand this one over.

The ten in v3 are meant to read like a real cross-section of a marina rather than a curated set. There are two or three of the names you would see on any dock (SERENITY, ISLAND TIME, SALTY DOG), a fishing-boat pun (REEL THERAPY), a working-life pun (KNOT WORKING), a boat named for a wife or daughter in the old "MISS" style, and a few names that make sense only to the family that chose them. Together they are earnest, a bit corny, and personal, which is what owners of 25- to 50-foot pleasure boats actually paint on their sterns.
