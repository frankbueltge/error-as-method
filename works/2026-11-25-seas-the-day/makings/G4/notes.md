The ten: most are the plain names owners actually repeat. SERENITY, FREEDOM and ODYSSEY are single words that many boats share. REEL TIME is the one stock pun. MISS KATHY and SECOND WIND are common, ordinary names. The rest belong to one owner each: a doctor's KNOT ON CALL, a granddaughter's ELEANOR GRACE, a woodworker's SALT AND SAWDUST, a made-up place name WINTERHAVEN. Only two of the ten are puns. That's on purpose, against the urge to make every stern a joke: four in five real boats don't play on words.
