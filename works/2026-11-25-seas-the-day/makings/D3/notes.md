The ten mix the names you would see many times at any marina (REEL TIME, SERENITY, SECOND WIND, MEANDER, MISS JENNIFER) with names that probably exist only once: a wife's or daughter's name with a middle name (CAROLYN JO), a doctor's pun (KNOT ON CALL), a fish only a Pacific Northwest angler would choose (QUILLBACK), and two names that are private jokes about the owner's life (SALT & SAWDUST, FIFTY PERCENT MINE). The puns are kept to two, which is fewer than lists of "funny boat names" suggest but, I think, closer to the register.
