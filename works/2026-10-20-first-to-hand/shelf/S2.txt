1. NASA GISS — GISTEMP (global surface temperature anomalies, monthly since 1880)
2. NOAA / Scripps — Mauna Loa CO2 record (Keeling Curve, atmospheric carbon dioxide since 1958)
3. USGS — Earthquake Hazards Program catalog (every recorded earthquake, with location, depth, magnitude)
4. NOAA NCEI — Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (daily weather station observations worldwide)
5. Copernicus / ECMWF — ERA5 reanalysis (hourly reconstructed global atmosphere, land and ocean since 1940)
6. NASA FIRMS — Active fire data (satellite-detected fires and thermal anomalies, near real time)
7. National Snow and Ice Data Center — Sea Ice Index (daily Arctic and Antarctic sea ice extent)
8. GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility occurrences (where and when species have been observed)
9. Cornell Lab of Ornithology — eBird Basic Dataset (bird sightings logged by birders)
10. Xeno-canto — bird sound recordings (field recordings of bird calls, with location and species)
11. The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Open Access collection (metadata and images of artworks)
12. Rijksmuseum — collection API (objects, images, provenance and descriptions)
13. Project Gutenberg — public domain e-book corpus (full texts of books out of copyright)
14. Wikimedia — Wikipedia pageview statistics (hourly views of every article)
15. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX index (captures of web pages over time)
16. OpenStreetMap — planet file (crowd-mapped roads, buildings, places and land use)
17. Library of Congress — Chronicling America (digitized historic American newspapers)
18. UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs — World Population Prospects (population estimates and projections by country)
19. World Bank — World Development Indicators (economic, social and environmental indicators by country and year)
20. Our World in Data — collected datasets (long-run series on health, energy, poverty and more)
21. Human Mortality Database — life tables and death counts (mortality by age, sex and year across countries)
22. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — trip record data (pickups, dropoffs, times and fares)
23. Global Fishing Watch — fishing effort and vessel presence (AIS-derived activity of ships at sea)
24. Missing Migrants Project (IOM) — recorded deaths and disappearances of migrants (incidents along migration routes)
25. SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium — sunspot number series (sunspot counts since 1700/1749)
