1. USGS — Earthquake Hazards Program catalog (time, location, depth and magnitude of every recorded earthquake worldwide)
2. NASA GISS — GISTEMP (monthly global surface temperature anomalies since 1880)
3. NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory — Mauna Loa CO2 record (daily and monthly atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration since 1958)
4. SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium — International Sunspot Number (daily and monthly sunspot counts since 1749)
5. The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Open Access collection (metadata and public-domain images for hundreds of thousands of artworks)
6. Wikimedia Foundation — Pageview statistics (hourly views of every Wikipedia article)
7. Project Gutenberg — eBook corpus (full texts of public-domain books)
8. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — Trip Record Data (pickup and dropoff times, locations and fares for taxi rides)
9. Cornell Lab of Ornithology / eBird — eBird Basic Dataset (bird sightings logged by observers worldwide)
10. GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility occurrences (where and when species have been observed or collected)
11. Missing Migrants Project, IOM — Missing Migrants dataset (deaths and disappearances of people on migration routes)
12. Global Forest Watch / Hansen et al. — Global Forest Change (annual tree cover loss and gain per 30 m pixel)
13. NASA FIRMS — Active fire data (satellite-detected fire hotspots, near real time)
14. OpenStreetMap — Planet file (crowd-mapped roads, buildings and places on Earth)
15. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX index (captures of web pages over time)
16. NASA Exoplanet Archive — Planetary Systems table (confirmed exoplanets and their orbital and physical properties)
17. Minor Planet Center — MPC Orbit database (orbits of asteroids and comets)
18. Freesound — sound archive (Creative Commons field recordings and sound samples with tags)
19. Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum — Collection data (objects with metadata, including dominant colours)
20. US Social Security Administration — Baby Names (frequency of first names given to US newborns since 1880)
21. UCDP, Uppsala University — Georeferenced Event Dataset (individual events of organised violence and their fatalities)
22. Our World in Data — Life expectancy (life expectancy at birth by country over time)
23. Copernicus / ESA — Sentinel-2 imagery (multispectral satellite images of Earth's land surface)
24. National Snow and Ice Data Center — Sea Ice Index (daily Arctic and Antarctic sea ice extent since 1978)
25. Lindsey et al. / Common Crawl — Common Crawl corpus (raw crawled web pages, monthly snapshots)
