1. NOAA NCEI — Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (daily temperature, precipitation and snowfall readings from weather stations worldwide)
2. USGS — Earthquake Hazards Program catalog (time, location, depth and magnitude of every recorded earthquake)
3. NASA GISS — GISTEMP surface temperature analysis (monthly global temperature anomalies since 1880)
4. Scripps Institution of Oceanography / NOAA — Mauna Loa CO2 record, the Keeling Curve (atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration measured since 1958)
5. Cornell Lab of Ornithology — eBird Basic Dataset (bird sightings logged by birdwatchers, with species, place and time)
6. Xeno-canto — bird and wildlife sound recordings (field recordings of animal vocalizations, with location metadata)
7. GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility occurrence records (where and when specimens and observations of living species were recorded)
8. The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Open Access collection (object metadata and public-domain images of the museum's holdings)
9. Rijksmuseum — collection API (artworks, makers, dates, materials and high-resolution images)
10. Project Gutenberg — public-domain e-books (full texts of tens of thousands of books)
11. Wikimedia Foundation — Wikipedia pageview and edit history dumps (how often articles are read and every revision made to them)
12. OpenStreetMap — planet file (crowd-mapped roads, buildings, paths and places on Earth)
13. NASA — Black Marble night lights, VIIRS (satellite images of artificial light on Earth at night)
14. ESA / Copernicus — Sentinel-2 imagery (multispectral satellite images of the Earth's land surface)
15. Minor Planet Center — orbit and observation database (asteroids and comets, with their discovery and orbital elements)
16. ESA — Gaia data release (positions, distances, motions and brightness of more than a billion stars)
17. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — trip record data (pickup and dropoff times, places and fares of taxi rides)
18. NYC Open Data — 311 service requests (complaints residents phone in about noise, potholes, heat and the like)
19. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX index (when web pages were captured and what changed over time)
20. Library of Congress — Chronicling America (digitized historical American newspaper pages with OCR text)
21. U.S. National Archives / Slave Voyages consortium — Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database (documented slaving voyages, ships, ports and people transported)
22. UNHCR — Refugee Data Finder (numbers of refugees, asylum seekers and displaced people by origin and host country)
23. Our World in Data — collected datasets (long-run statistics on population, health, energy, food and poverty)
24. Global Fishing Watch — fishing effort data (vessel activity at sea inferred from AIS ship-tracking signals)
25. MusicBrainz — open music encyclopedia database (artists, releases, recordings and their relationships)
