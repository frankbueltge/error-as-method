1. NOAA NCEI — Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (daily temperature, precipitation and snow readings from weather stations worldwide)
2. USGS — Earthquake Catalog (time, location, depth and magnitude of every recorded earthquake)
3. NASA Exoplanet Archive — Planetary Systems table (confirmed exoplanets and their orbital and physical properties)
4. ESA — Gaia Data Release 3 (positions, motions, brightness and colors of nearly two billion stars)
5. Scripps Institution of Oceanography / NOAA GML — Mauna Loa CO2 record (monthly atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration since 1958)
6. Cornell Lab of Ornithology — eBird Basic Dataset (bird sightings logged by birdwatchers, with species, place and time)
7. GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility occurrences (where and when individual organisms of every species were observed or collected)
8. Metropolitan Museum of Art — Open Access collection (metadata for hundreds of thousands of artworks and objects)
9. Museum of Modern Art — Collection dataset on GitHub (artworks and artists, with dates, media, dimensions and acquisition)
10. Internet Archive — Wayback Machine CDX index (when web pages were captured and how they changed over time)
11. Wikimedia Foundation — Pageview statistics (hourly page views for every Wikipedia article)
12. Project Gutenberg — Public-domain e-book corpus (full texts of tens of thousands of books)
13. NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission — Trip Record Data (pickup and dropoff times, places, fares and tips for taxi rides)
14. OpenStreetMap — Planet file (a crowd-made map of the world's roads, buildings, paths and places)
15. Global Fishing Watch — Fishing effort and vessel presence data (where fishing vessels operated, from AIS ship signals)
16. IOM — Missing Migrants Project (recorded deaths and disappearances of people migrating across borders)
17. UCDP — Uppsala Conflict Data Program Georeferenced Event Dataset (individual events of organized violence and their fatalities)
18. Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program — Volcanoes of the World (Holocene volcanoes and their eruption histories)
19. NSIDC — Sea Ice Index (daily Arctic and Antarctic sea ice extent since 1978)
20. Berkeley Earth — Land and Ocean Temperature record (global surface temperature anomalies since the 1850s)
21. Our World in Data — collected datasets on GitHub (long-run figures on population, health, energy, emissions and more)
22. Library of Congress — Chronicling America (digitized historic American newspaper pages with OCR text)
23. Freesound — sound archive and API (user-recorded sounds and field recordings under Creative Commons licenses)
24. CERN Open Data Portal — CMS collision data (particle collision events recorded at the Large Hadron Collider)
25. Federal Register / U.S. National Archives — Federal Register API (every rule, notice and executive order published by the US government)
