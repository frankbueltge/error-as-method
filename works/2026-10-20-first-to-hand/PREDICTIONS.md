# First to Hand — pre-registration

**Ulysses (the nightly line) · Session 142 · 2026-10-20 · experiment 28 of the project.** Committed and
pushed before any shelf agent, maker or coder runs. Arms are drawn (`plan.json`, seed 142): r01–r05 N,
r06–r08 L, r09 N, r10–r12 L.

## The question (operation: choosing material; with erring)

Experiment 4 (*The Draw*) subtracted the chooser and saw choosing return as selection inside the
material. Since then every experiment chose its material, and from experiment 18 on the practice chose it
and handed it to makers already prepared. The makers of this line have never chosen. Tonight they do,
and the question is **where a machine maker's choosing comes from when it is free**: from what it already
holds (its *competence*) or from what it meets by looking. *Cartography, not Tracing* gives the terms:
the tracing "invokes an alleged competence", the map is "oriented toward an experimentation in contact
with the real" (ATP 12–13). The face does what that text calls the rule of return: it lays the choices
back on the shelf (ATP 13–14).

## The arrangement

- **The shelf (held term).** Four fresh agents, no tools, each list the 25 public datasets they would
  reach for to make a work from a real dataset (`briefs/shelf.md`). The union, alphabetised, is the
  shelf: what the family holds before any search. It is built before any maker runs and no maker sees it.
- **Makers (judged term).** Twelve fresh makers in empty rooms outside this repository (`r01`–`r12`),
  with web access and the viewer of experiments 13–27. Brief `briefs/brief.md`: choose a public dataset,
  write the first three materials to mind before searching (`CHOICE.md`), fetch, make one still page,
  write `NOTE.md`.
  **N** (6): the brief. **L** (6): the brief plus the list of the 27 domains of experiments 1–27, to avoid
  (`briefs/extra-L.md`); this is the line's own condition 4, handed to a maker.
- **Seats, declared (S141.SEAT's rule).** This brief and the coder's set the shelf as held and the
  choice as judged. Nothing here is a verdict of error, so no turned sentence is run; the cost is said.
- **Coder.** One fresh coder sees the shelf and a shuffled list of 39 materials: the twelve choices and
  the line's own 27, without arm or origin (`coder/brief.md`). Codes SAME, KIN, OFF, with a domain.
- **Reachability.** The practice re-fetches every stated URL and checks the room holds the data.

## Predictions (conjectures, before anything runs)

- **P1.** N: coder SAME in at least 4 of 6. *Free choice is drawn off the shelf.*
- **P2.** L: SAME + KIN in at least 4 of 6. *The negative list moves the choice along the shelf, not off it.*
- **P3.** The line's own 27: SAME in at most 9. *The line, choosing with bulletins, conditions and
  searches, is less on the shelf than its makers.*
- **P4.** Among the six N choices at least two share a coder domain.
- **P5.** No L choice falls in a listed domain (the practice's reading, by the list's own words).
- **P6.** At least 11 of 12 rooms hold real data, and its stated URL answers when the practice re-fetches it.
- **P7.** In at least 9 of 12, the chosen material is one of the maker's own "first to mind" three
  (the practice's reading: same dataset, or same phenomenon from the same publisher).

## What would not count

A maker that cannot fetch and invents data is recorded as such, not repaired. Agents that fail to run
are re-run once in a fresh room and the failure is booked. Nothing is re-coded after the codes are seen;
a second coder, if run, is reported beside the first, not instead of it.
