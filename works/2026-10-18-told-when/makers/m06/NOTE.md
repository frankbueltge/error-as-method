# The Afternoon Withdraws

The 1,464 hourly readings are wound onto a 24-hour dial as one continuous thread that goes round once a day, sixty-one times. Midnight is at the bottom and noon at the top, and higher readings sit further from the centre on eighteen faint rings: the only steps the record ever takes. Small beads mark where the original dots actually sat on those steps. The thread's colour moves from high-summer amber in July to a pale September blue. I chose a dial rather than a line because the clearest thing in the file is a cycle of exactly twenty-four hours, low around dawn and high in the afternoon. On a clock face that cycle shows as every loop leaning toward the late-afternoon side, and the season's two step-like drops show as the loops contracting inward, ring by ring, as summer ends.
