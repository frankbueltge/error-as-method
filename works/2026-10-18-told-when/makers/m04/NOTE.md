# Sixty-One Turns

The record is wound onto a 24-hour clock face, one ring per day. 15 July is the outer ring and 13 September is the centre. Each hour fills one sector in one of 18 stepped tones, because the measurement itself only ever takes 18 discrete steps. I chose the clock because the file holds two motions that a straight timeline blurs together. One is a daily breath: the value is lowest around 08:00 and highest around 19:00, and on the disk this lines up from ring to ring as a cool wedge on the morning side and a warm one on the evening side. The other is a slow slide of about ten steps over the two months. Spiralled inward, that slide reads as the summer's straw-coloured rim darkening into blue toward the centre. A few sudden drops show up as hard edges between neighbouring rings.
