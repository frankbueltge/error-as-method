# Sixty-One Turns

The 1,464 hourly values are wound into a single coil, one turn per day, with the first day at the centre and the last at the rim. Each hour of the day always sits at the same bearing, so the daily rhythm and the slow seasonal change appear together in one still image: the slow change shows as rings from the centre outward (a steady stretch, a sharp drop, a brief recovery, then a long fall), and the daily rhythm shows as the warm field sagging toward the afternoon side of every turn. I chose the coil because a straight line hides the day inside the season, and a grid of days cuts the hours apart at midnight. The spiral keeps the record continuous, as it was kept, and still lines the days up against each other. The values were recorded in whole steps, eighteen in all, so the work uses exactly eighteen colours and never blends between them.
