# Eighteen Picks

The record turned out to take only eighteen distinct values, so the work treats each one as a row of weft in a hanging cloth, with the sixty-one days running across it as the warp. Wherever the record touched a value, a coloured float is brocaded over the plain linen ground, and it is denser where the dots were denser. Read from left to right, the band of colour steps down the cloth in plateaus and sudden drops. Its daily swing shows as the regular breaks in each float. The dye shifts from madder red at the highest row to indigo at the lowest, so the descent reads as a cooling as well as a fall. I chose cloth because the material was already a grid of separate threads rather than a smooth line. Weaving keeps the record's coarse steps and its daily rhythm as they are, with nothing smoothed or invented. One tassel per day hangs from the bottom edge to count the time.
