# Eighteen Rings

When I read the picture, the main thing it showed was that the quantity never varies smoothly. It only ever sits on one of eighteen evenly spaced values, and over the sixty-one days it steps down through them while rising and falling once each day. The work winds the record into a spiral of one turn per day, read clockwise from the top, and turns each of the eighteen values into a ring. For each hour the line follows a ring, and on the hour it steps sideways to the next one. Inside every ring, July sits on the outer edge and September on the inner, and the ink shifts from brown to blue-black over the season. Read this way, the record draws its own diagram. The daily rise pushes every turn out to one side, so the rings all bulge toward the same hours. The long decline of the season moves the line inward, ring by ring, in sudden drops instead of a gentle slope. The hourly order comes from my reconstruction of a 300-pixel-wide image, where each pixel covers about five hours. The rings themselves are what the picture shows.
