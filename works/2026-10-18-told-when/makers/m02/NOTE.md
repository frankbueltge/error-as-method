# Sixty-One Rows

The record is knitted: one stitch for every hour and one row of 24 stitches for every day. The piece is laid on its side so that, as in the original plot, it starts at the left and runs to the right. The thread ends hang loose at both edges. The original values sit on only eighteen distinct levels, so the piece uses exactly eighteen yarns, ordered from deep blue (lowest) to dark red (highest). Under the piece, each yarn appears as a ball sized by how many stitches it made. I chose a knitted blanket because its own rules match the data closely: it can only show the levels it has yarn for, it grows one hour at a time, and each day becomes a row. Read across the rows, the daily rise shows as a warm band across the middle hours. The two-month descent, with its pauses and sudden drops, becomes the cloth slowly running out of warm colours.
