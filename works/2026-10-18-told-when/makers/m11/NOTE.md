# Sixty-One Turns

The 1,464 hours are wound into one spiral of 61 turns. Each turn is one day, starting at the centre on 15 July and moving outward to 13 September, and each of its 24 segments is one hour, with midnight at the top. The colour of a segment is the value's step, out of eighteen, running from cold blue to ember orange. In the source plot the record looks like a slope with some fine fuzz on it. Winding it like a clock turns that fuzz into a daily cycle: hours on the same side of the day sit on the same side of the spiral, so a warmer sector of the afternoon shows up in every ring. The slow decline becomes a core that cools from the inside out, with the warm spells and sudden drops left as bands across it. I picked the spiral because it shows both scales at the same moment, the day and the season, without either one hiding the other.
