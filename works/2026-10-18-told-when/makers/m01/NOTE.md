# Eighteen Steps Down

The record could only ever stand on one of eighteen evenly spaced levels, so the work makes those levels literal steps. It folds the 1,464 hours into 61 columns, one per day. For each day it lays a tread on every step the reading touched, and the tread is darker the longer the reading stayed there. The empty bands between treads are as much a part of the work as the marks: they are where the instrument could not see. The daily swing shows in the height of each column. The descent over two months shows as a staircase. The few sudden multi-day drops, and the partial recoveries after them, show as landings. I chose this over redrawing the curve because the coarse quantization, the daily breathing and the slow fall are the three true things the picture holds, and a stair carries all three without pretending to more precision than the source has.
