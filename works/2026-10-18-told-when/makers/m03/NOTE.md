# Nine Weeks Down

The 61 days are set out as a wall calendar, Monday to Sunday, from Tuesday 15 July to Saturday 13 September 2025. Each square holds one day of the record, with its hours running left to right. Within each square, the 18 fixed steps the record sits on are drawn as a faint column of dots, and ink falls where the original picture had ink. Grey ink marks places where the small JPEG is blurred, rather than a guessed value. I chose the calendar because the record has two rhythms, and a long strip blurs them together. Folding it into days and weeks keeps them apart. Across each row you see the same rise and fall every day. Down the page you see the whole band sink, week by week, toward the bottom of its squares. Any day that breaks the pattern, such as a midday collapse or a flat grey day, stands out against its neighbours.
