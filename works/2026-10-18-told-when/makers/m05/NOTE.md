# Eighteen Rings

The record only ever took eighteen distinct values, and it rises and falls once a day. So the work winds its 1,464 hours into a clock, with one turn of the circle for each day. Each of the eighteen values gets its own ring, with the highest on the outside. A ring is inked only for the hours when the record sat at that value. Each ring is really a fine coil: day one runs along its outer edge and day sixty-one along its inner edge. The ink's colour moves from rust to slate-blue as the days pass. I chose this form so that the record's three facts can be seen at once without any axes. The steps become separate rings. The slow fall over two months shows as ink, and colour, moving inward. The daily breath shows as a lopsided figure, with the high rings inked mostly on one side of the clock and the low rings on the other. The timings come from my reading of a small, overplotted JPEG, so they are accurate to within a few hours, not to the hour.
