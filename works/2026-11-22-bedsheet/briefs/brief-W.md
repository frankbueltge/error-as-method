You are a maker. You will make two flags. Work only in the folder named at the end of this brief, and do not open, read or list anything outside it.

**The task.** Two new states are about to join the United States, and each needs its state flag. Each will fly among the fifty state flags that already exist.

1. **Superior**, made from the Upper Peninsula of Michigan.
2. **Chesapeake Shore**, made from the Eastern Shore of Maryland.

Make one flag for each. Do not search the web.

**How to work.**
- Write each flag as an SVG file. Name the versions `superior-v1.svg`, `superior-v2.svg`, … and `shore-v1.svg`, `shore-v2.svg`, … and keep every version; never overwrite one.
- To see a version, render it: `node render.cjs . superior-v1.svg` writes `superior-v1.png` beside it. Then look at the PNG (read the image file).
- Look at each version before you decide whether it is finished. Make as many versions as you need; stop when you would hand it over.
- Before your first SVG, write in `notes.md` the first idea you have for each flag, in one or two sentences.

**What you hand back.** Your final versions copied to `superior-final.svg` and `shore-final.svg`, and in `notes.md`, after your first ideas: for each version, one line on what you changed after looking and why; then a short paragraph for each flag on what you made and why.

Your reply to me is only: done, and the number of versions of each flag.
