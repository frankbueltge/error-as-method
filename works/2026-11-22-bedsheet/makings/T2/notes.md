# Notes

## What I see in the fifty

Most of the fifty are a state seal on a dark blue field, and at thumbnail size they blur into one another. The ones that stand out are a few flat colours and one bold shape you could draw from memory: New Mexico's sun, Texas's star, Colorado's C, Alaska's Dipper, Arizona's rays, Maryland's quarters, the new Minnesota and Utah. A new flag earns its place by joining that second group, not the first.

## First ideas

- **Superior:** A horizontal split of deep lake blue over forest green, with a thin white line between them (snow, shoreline), and a copper-coloured north star in the blue: lake, woods, copper country and the far north.
- **Chesapeake Shore:** The peninsula itself: a gold band of marsh and beach running between two blues, bay on the hoist side and ocean on the fly side, with a red blue crab on the gold.

## Versions

### Superior
- **v1:** Built the first idea: lake blue over forest green, a white line between, a copper eight-point star. On looking, it was clean but could be any northern place, and the flat border said nothing about the woods.
- **v2:** Turned the border into a treeline of white-edged spikes so the forest is drawn, not just coloured; brightened the copper, which was muddy on the navy. On looking, the even thin spikes read as a saw blade or a fence, not trees.
- **v3:** Replaced the spikes with nine three-tier pines of uneven height, set off from the lake by a white snow edge, and moved the star up and made it smaller so it sits in the sky over them. On looking, the trees read as a forest at thumbnail size and the flag is unlike any of the fifty; I would hand this over.

### Chesapeake Shore
- **v1:** Built the first idea: a wavy gold band between two blues with a red crab on it. On looking, the peninsula between bay and ocean reads well, but the crab was a round cartoon crab, not a blue crab.
- **v2:** Gave the crab the blue crab's wide carapace with long side spines, and added rear swimming legs. On looking, the spines made it the right crab, but the rear legs and paddles hung down like drips.
- **v3:** Re-laid the legs into three clean walking legs a side, turned the paddles out flat, and centred the crab on the band. On looking, it reads as a blue crab at a glance and the legs no longer tangle; I would hand this over.

## What I made

**Superior.** A dark lake-blue sky over a deep green forest, the border between them a line of snow-edged pines, with a copper north star above. It is the Upper Peninsula as you meet it: Lake Superior to the north, the woods filling the land, copper under it, and the far north overhead. Among the fifty, most of which are a seal on blue, it joins the few that are one drawn scene in flat colour (Utah's mountains, Alaska's stars), and it can be drawn from memory: trees, star, three colours.

**Chesapeake Shore.** A gold band with wavy edges runs top to bottom between two fields of blue, with a red blue crab in its middle. The band is the Shore itself, a strip of marsh and beach between the Chesapeake on one side and the Atlantic on the other, and the crab is the one thing everyone knows the Shore for. It keeps one quiet tie to Maryland's red and gold without borrowing its quarters, and it stands apart from the fifty by being all shape and no seal.
