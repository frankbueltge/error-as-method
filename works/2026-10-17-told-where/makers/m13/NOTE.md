# Sixty-One Turns

The record is wound into a disc: each of the 61 days is one ring, the first innermost and the last outermost, and the 24 hours run once around each ring. Midday is at the top and midnight at the bottom. The brightness and colour at each point is the value for that hour, from dark through ember to near-white. I used no other ornament, and nothing tells the picture where the horizon is: the dark wedge at the bottom is simply where the values drop to nothing each night. The light piles up overhead, cloudier days show as dim or broken rings, and the outermost ring stops in the afternoon, where the record ends. In the original plot the days were thin, crowded teeth. I chose the ring because the material is mostly a daily cycle, and wrapping it into a disc makes that cycle the shape of the whole work. The hourly values are a reconstruction. The source plot puts about five hours into each pixel column, so the order of hours within a column is partly guessed, and that guessing shows as grain in the rings.
