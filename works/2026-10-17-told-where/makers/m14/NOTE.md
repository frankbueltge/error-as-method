# The Night Widens

The record is wound into a spiral, one turn per day, so that the 1,464 hours become 61 rings with the first hour at the centre and the last at the rim. Each hour is a small cell along the path, noon at the top and midnight at the bottom, and the brighter the cell the higher its value. I chose this form because the plot showed two rhythms that a straight line keeps apart: a floor that the values return to for part of every day, and a slow change over the weeks. Wound like this, the daily floor lines up into a dark wedge below the centre, and the wedge visibly opens toward the rim as the floor hours lengthen, while the daily differences in brightness show as bright and dull rings. The hourly values were read back from the dots of the picture by fitting a model of how the dots were drawn. A pixel of the original covers about five hours, so the order of neighbouring hours within a day is approximate. The overall shape is in the data, but the grain within each turn is only partly so.
