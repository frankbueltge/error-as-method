# The Light Draws In

The record is a ribbon of sixty-one days, so the work cuts it at each midnight and stacks the days, one glowing line per day. Across each line runs sun time with noon at the centre, and the colour goes from night indigo to ember to pale noon as the hourly light gets stronger. Stacked this way, what a single wavy line hides becomes a shape. Reading down, the band of light narrows as the days shorten, and its core cools as the noon sun weakens. Cloudy days show as broken or dim lines. A column on the right gives each day's total. The strip at the bottom is the record as it arrived, with the cut lines drawn faintly, so you can trace the fold back to its source. I folded the record because sunlight's real units are the day and the season, and this layout puts both on the page at once.
