# Sixty-One Turns

The record is wound into a spiral with one turn per day, the first day at the centre and the last at the rim, so the 1,464 hours become one continuous line and each hour of the day sits at the same angle on every turn. The value sets the brightness: high hours glow pale gold, and the hours when the record rests on its floor go dark. I chose this form because the flat plot hides what is strongest in the data, a floor that comes back every 24 hours and a burst of high values between floors. Turned so each day's floor faces down, the days stack into a lit fan above a dark wedge. Within it you can see the dim, broken turns of poor days and the bright turns of good ones, and the last weeks dim toward the rim. The source picture has only about five pixels per day, so each turn is about as sharp as the record allows: soft over spans of a few hours, not exact hour by hour.
