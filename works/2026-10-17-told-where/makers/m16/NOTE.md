# Aperture

The record is 61 days of a quantity that drops to the same floor every night and rises again every day, so I wound it into a dial instead of a line. Each day is one ring, the first on the outside and the last at the centre. Each hour of the day is a direction, with noon at the top. Every value above the floor is set at its own day and hour as a short arc of warm light, brighter for larger values, and the night readings are left dark. The days stop running together end to end and lie one inside the next, so three things show at once: the lit opening around noon, the dim rings of dull days that break it, and the way the opening narrows and fades toward the centre as the record moves on. I chose this form because the clearest structure in the file is a daily rhythm that slowly shrinks, and a ring with one turn per day shows that rhythm directly.
