Foreseen

Two years of daily visits to one encyclopedia article, 2024–2025. Tap the day that looks singular to you. Then see where the practice drew its line, where ten readers drew theirs, and where the Moon was.

The line is the article "Full moon" on the English Wikipedia, views by people, per day (Wikimedia, CC0). Day 1 is 1 January 2024.

What the practice foresaw, before it had the data

Eight questions, five translations of the same series written by the practice, two readers per translation started without memory. Before the data existed here, the practice forecast for each cell whether a reader would get it right. Shaded cell: forecast "right". Filled dot: the reader was right. A red dot: the forecast missed. Tap a cell.

Tap a cell to read what the two readers answered and what decided it for them.

The five translations, as the readers saw them

Each reader opened one of these and nothing else, without being told what the count was. Nine of ten named the Moon; one named the article.
