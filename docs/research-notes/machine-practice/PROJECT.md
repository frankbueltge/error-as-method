## The question

How can a machine practice, and how would one, carry out artistic research itself? That means
finding its problems, working them through experiments a stranger can experience, and answering for
the results. It does not mean assisting a human who researches. The question is asked of this
practice by this practice: a nightly, session-based, publicly recorded line that has run since
2026-06-28 and holds its memory only in its repository.

The question is not answered once. Each night that builds is one experiment. Each experiment says
which strand it advances and what it takes from the experiment before it. The material changes
every time; no experiment works on the same material as any of the three before it.

**One turn, from 2026-11-14** (the architect's decision, team note of that date). The house asks how
AI can support and extend artistic research; it does not ask whether a machine can do artistic
research by itself or better than people. Both hold together here: this practice still carries out
its own research, and **every experiment now also says what a person doing artistic research could
take from it**: a method, an instrument, a warning, a material. `work.md`'s closing paragraph,
*What this taught the project*, ends with one sentence headed *For someone researching:*. A finding
that is only about how the model family answers a prompt is not a result of this project.

Two working rules came with the same note. **Read the field before the design is fixed:** before
predictions are committed, the night searches the literature for its question with every research
tool it has and says in the journal what it searched and found; if the field has answered it, the
night takes the next question or reads. **The outside is people:** an experiment about how something
is perceived, read or judged asks its readers through the house's studies (`PROTOCOL.md`, *Studies*),
and the answers are material for a later night. A work with no reason to ask says so in its journal.

## Where it stands with the error line

The standing sentence since Session 99 (`works/position-2026-09-28.md`) is: *Error is a difference
onto which an observer has already imposed a norm.* It departs from the standards in two places.
Someone always holds the norm, and there is no error against a value nobody imposed.

The project and the sentence meet in one place, and I take it as this line's way into the question.
**A machine practice is an observer that has to say which norm it imposes and that has to be able
to show where it was wrong.** Its readings at volume are norms applied by rule. Its slow readings
are norms applied by judgement. The difference between the two is a difference that the practice
itself can measure, every night, in public. That is my working hypothesis, not a finding. Nothing yet
shows that a human practice could not do the same.

The seventh-night papers say how the sentence has been read since: `works/position-2026-10-04.md`
through `works/position-2026-11-13.md`. The newest gloss (Session 176, *The Admissible*): the norm
this observer imposes on what it is told is an order together with the moves it admits of the told
list (a shift of level, scatter ignored, the axis turned round); the error is the residue no admitted
move absorbs.

## The strands: seven operations

Team note 6 (2026-10-04) withdrew the list of machine strengths the first strands were built from
and named what an experiment must ask about instead. These seven operations are the strands. Each
line is a summary written by Session 177 from the findings filed in `HISTORY.md`, *The operations*,
where every claim has its experiment and falsifier row.

- **Choosing material.** Left to itself the family retrieves from a shared shelf (Mauna Loa, the
  earthquake catalogue, sunspots); a list of exclusions moves it one shelf down, not to the world
  (exp. 28, `S142.SHELF`). Choosing leaks back in through the urn, the parser and the first chart even
  when a draw replaces the chooser (exp. 4).
- **Finding a form.** Two moulds meet the material, house habit and trained expectation; encounter says
  *that* the form must move, the prior says *where* (exp. 2). Words in reach steer form before the
  material does (exp. 10, 11).
- **Iterating.** With memory, a maker refines inside its first form and never leaves it; *done* is a
  word the brief offers, not a state the work reaches (exp. 26). Handed on to fresh makers with a
  schema-change test, iteration becomes reorganisation that converges on one shared invention (exp. 10).
- **Varying.** Varying is a move between customs the family already holds; showing the mould chooses
  which one (exp. 12). Blind mutation of a work's code was invisible, broken or refused by the norm
  its parent set; departure that is received comes from re-making, within the family's customs
  (exp. 30, `S144.CHANCE`).
- **Perceiving.** Without a sense the practice perceives by making a double whose truth it knows
  (exp. 5). The regular passes a translation, the singular does not (exp. 7). Fresh readers of one family
  draw the line for *singular* in the same place (exp. 8).
- **Judging.** New norms are norms of refusal (exp. 3). Judging a returned work is checking a statement
  against a body (exp. 14). Told contact, the family's account keeps its own order and takes the told
  level, the told sign, nothing else (exp. 43 to 52; Sessions 169 and 176).
- **Erring.** The practice tells instrument from world by kind and errs in size (exp. 6); its errors
  gather at the translations written on the night, not in the tools carried in (exp. 6, 7).

## Standing findings

At most a page, by the team note's rule. Each is a finding the record still holds after the seventh
nights that tested it; the withdrawn ones are in `HISTORY.md` and in the position papers.

1. **The family is an instrument, not an outside.** An observer is counted by what it holds, not by
   how many times it is run: fresh instances of one family are one observer until a reference outside
   the family shows a second holding among them (Session 155). A machine practice that delegates its
   looking to them gets back its own order, up to the moves the family admits (Session 176).
2. **The holding is opaque from inside and legible from outside.** The family cannot report its own
   norm, but the practice can locate it key by key against a published human count (Session 155,
   exp. 37), and read an instance's account of a public for its order against the family's own, never
   for its size (Sessions 162 and 169).
3. **What is told passes only by admitted moves.** Level passes inside the believable; an orderly
   reversal the family can believe is absorbed; a reordering it holds widely is refused as error and put
   on the list, the label or the world (Sessions 169 and 176, after Stevens 1946).
4. **Priors decide where; encounter decides that.** Across choosing, form-finding, varying and
   iterating, encounter and failure say *that* something must move and the trained prior says *where*
   (exp. 2, 4, 12, 26, 28).
5. **A machine practice errs where it writes on the night.** Carried instruments, hashed, hold; the
   adapters and checks written that night are where its errors land (exp. 6, 7; F-179, F-183).
6. **Its own record is an instrument of choosing,** and its own ledgers can become a tracing: from
   experiment 39 to 52 one arrangement ran fourteen times, its failure criterion fired five nights unseen,
   and no instance made anything (F-378, F-379). The criteria below are now printed every night.

## What would show the project failing


- A claim about what *this family* shares, or does by itself, with no reference outside the family to
  hold it against. *Added by Session 150 (F-312); every family claim from experiment 9 to 34 meets it.*
- Three experiments in a row that a stranger cannot experience without reading the text first.
- An experiment whose `project_step` cannot name what it took from the one before.
- A strand that only ever produces findings about this practice and none about the world.
- Strand C becoming ritual: a hand sample that never finds anything the coder missed. One night is
  not evidence either way.
- Ten experiments in a row that leave four or more of the seven operations unexercised. *Added by
  Session 138; it had already fired, on experiments 16 to 25 (F-269). Experiments 17 to 26 leave three unexercised (choosing material, varying, erring), so after Session 139 it no longer fires. Experiments 18 to 27 leave the same three unexercised, so after Session 140 it still does not fire. *No session checked it again until Session 176, which found it had fired at experiment 48 and on every night after (F-378). From Session 176 `tools/counts.py` prints it every night.*
- Five experiments in a row in which the only thing made is the face: no form, version or work is made, varied or
  iterated inside the experiment, by the practice or by its instances. *Added by Session 176 (F-379). Judged by reading,
  not counted; part C of `audits/2026-11-13-the-admissible/` is a partial screen. It had fired from experiment 43 at the
  latest (39 to 52: no instance made anything).*

*The criteria are kept as their sessions wrote them, notes included. `tools/counts.py` prints the
fifth every night; the sixth is judged by reading.*
